Quentin Tarantino Movies, Ranked

10 films, ranked on my own star ratings by Arwin Edward Bagaslao. Updated 2026-05-05.

My Tarantino ranking is basically a violence-and-dialogue buffet, but I am not here only for the cool needle drops and blood spray. I care about momentum, characters talking like every conversation is a duel, revenge that feels operatic, an

  1. 1
    Django Unchained (2012)
    ★★★★★

    From the opening moments, Quentin Tarantino’s cinema confidence is undeniable.

  2. 2
    Kill Bill: Vol. 1 (2003)
    ★★★★★

    In my memory, this felt like a fast paced action film, but on rewatch it plays more like a slow burn.

  3. 3
    Inglourious Basterds (2009)
    ★★★★★

    The opening score sets the tone right away.

  4. 4
    Death Proof (2007)
    ★★★★½

    So this is how it feels when Quentin Tarantino directs a Fast and Furious movie?

  5. 5
    Once Upon a Time... in Hollywood (2019)
    ★★★★

    A laid back and nostalgic Tarantino film that focuses more on atmosphere, character hangouts, and tension simmering beneath the surface rather than constant action.

  6. 6
    Jackie Brown (1997)
    ★★★½

    I immediately felt Tarantino’s signature rhythm in how tension is built through conversations, framing, and quiet maneuvering instead of nonstop action.

  7. 7
    Kill Bill: Vol. 2 (2004)
    ★★★½

    Kill Bill: Vol. 2 takes a very different approach from its predecessor, trading constant spectacle for restraint, dialogue, and character driven tension.

  8. 8
    Pulp Fiction (1994)
    ★★★

    Pulp Fiction leans heavily into dialogue driven storytelling, prioritizing long conversations and character interactions over traditional action or spectacle.

  9. 9
    Reservoir Dogs (1992)
    ★½

    It felt like Among Us, nobody knows who the rat is and paranoia slowly poisons the room.

  10. 10
    The Hateful Eight (2015)
    ★

    Binalikan ko ulit ito to give it another chance after rewatching Django Unchained, hoping na magugustuhan ko na siya this time.
