Elizabeth Debicki Movies, Ranked

Ice, Elegance, and Stolen Scenes

8 films, ranked on my own star ratings by Arwin Edward Bagaslao. Updated 2026-11-26.

She is usually the coolest thing in the frame, running a time war in Tenet, playing the villain in The Man from U.N.C.L.E., floating through The Great Gatsby. Ranked by my own stars, and a few of these films clearly did not deserve her.
