Al Pacino Movies, Ranked

Loud Eyes, Bigger Consequences

11 films, ranked on my own star ratings by Arwin Edward Bagaslao. Updated 2026-10-25.

An Al Pacino ranking from my watched films: loud eyes, louder choices, crime-family damage, and performances that can turn a normal room into a pressure cooker. Ranked by stars first, then by momentum, emotional consequence, rewatch pull, a

  1. 1
    Heat (1995)
    ★★★★★

    Cute ni Natalie Portman and ang galing agad niya umarte kahit bata pa siya dito.

  2. 2
    Insomnia (2002)
    ★★★★½

    I experience this as a tense psychological detective film where Nolan uses pacing, color, and performance to make me physically feel the character’s unraveling state.

  3. 3
    The Godfather (1972)
    ★★★★½

    This watch clicked for me in a way it didn’t before.

  4. 4
    Ocean's Thirteen (2007)
    ★★★★½
  5. 5
    The Godfather Part II (1974)
    ★★★★

    The Godfather Part II shifts away from loud violence into quiet power plays, where conversations, looks, and decisions carry more danger than gunfire.

  6. 6
    Once Upon a Time... in Hollywood (2019)
    ★★★★

    A laid back and nostalgic Tarantino film that focuses more on atmosphere, character hangouts, and tension simmering beneath the surface rather than constant action.

  7. 7
    Scarface (1983)
    ★★★★

    This feels like a raw, flashy rise and fall crime story carried by Al Pacino’s intense presence and a neon soaked Miami atmosphere.

  8. 8
    House of Gucci (2021)
    ★★★★
  9. 9
    The Godfather Part III (1990)
    ★★★½

    This feels like a story about legacy, aging, and the heavy cost of power.

  10. 10
    Carlito's Way (1993)
    ★★★½

    It still has that Scarface (1983) Miami club aesthetic.

  11. 11
    The Irishman (2019)
    ★½

    It has the same vibes as Goodfellas, The Godfather, and The Departed with its mafia centered story and criminal world.
