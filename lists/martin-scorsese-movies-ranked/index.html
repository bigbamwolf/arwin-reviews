Martin Scorsese Movies, Ranked

12 films, ranked on my own star ratings by Arwin Edward Bagaslao. Updated 2026-09-18.

I watch Martin Scorsese films like confessions. They’re loud, sinful, obsessive, and painfully honest. His characters chase power, money, faith, or redemption, and usually lose themselves along the way. This ranking is based on impact and r

  1. 1
    Shutter Island (2010)
    ★★★★★
  2. 2
    The Wolf of Wall Street (2013)
    ★★★★★
  3. 3
    The Last Temptation of Christ (1988)
    ★★★★★

    I don’t know why, but I felt really unmotivated to watch this.

  4. 4
    The Departed (2006)
    ★★★★½

    At first, akala ko coming of age movie siya.

  5. 5
    Silence (2016)
    ★★★★

    From the very first frames, Silence announces itself visually.

  6. 6
    Killers of the Flower Moon (2023)
    ★★★★

    Detective style mystery, may pagka Knives Out with the world vibe of Sherlock Holmes and the grit of Peaky Blinders.

  7. 7
    The Aviator (2004)
    ★★★½
  8. 8
    Taxi Driver (1976)
    ★★★

    Ang bata ni Robert De Niro dito. Parang batang Jake Gyllenhaal energy sa Donnie Darko.

  9. 9
    Raging Bull (1980)
    ★★

    Opening credits pa lang, ang ganda na agad ng framing.

  10. 10
    GoodFellas (1990)
    ★½

    A crime rise and fall story told directly from the main character’s perspective.

  11. 11
    The Irishman (2019)
    ★½

    It has the same vibes as Goodfellas, The Godfather, and The Departed with its mafia centered story and criminal world.

  12. 12
    Gangs of New York (2002)
    ★

    Reminds me of the scale, period sets, and wardrobe of Killers of the Flower Moon (2023).
