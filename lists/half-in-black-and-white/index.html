Half in Black and White

9 films, ranked on my own star ratings by Arwin Edward Bagaslao. Updated 2026-05-05.

Films that go monochrome for part of the runtime and colour for the rest. A timeline, a first act, a framing device, one sequence. The switch is the point, so none of these belong in a pure black and white list. Ordered by my rating. The fu

  1. 1
    Schindler's List (1993)
    ★★★★★

    On my first watch, I thought this was just about a capitalist exploiting people through his own version of forced labor.

  2. 2
    Kill Bill: Vol. 1 (2003)
    ★★★★★

    In my memory, this felt like a fast paced action film, but on rewatch it plays more like a slow burn.

  3. 3
    Pleasantville (1998)
    ★★★★½

    This as a clever film about waking up inside a reality that feels safe, controlled, and limited, then slowly realizing there is something more beyond it.

  4. 4
    Memento (2000)
    ★★★★½
  5. 5
    Poor Things (2023)
    ★★★★½

    Emma Stone completely disappears into the role.

  6. 6
    Oppenheimer (2023)
    ★★★★

    I forgot how haunting the score is right from the start.

  7. 7
    The French Dispatch of the Liberty, Kansas Evening Sun (2021)
    ★★★½

    I like this better among all of Wes’ works.

  8. 8
    Raging Bull (1980)
    ★★

    Opening credits pa lang, ang ganda na agad ng framing.

  9. 9
    Maestro (2023)
    ★½

    I suffered again from detaching from it because of the black and white.
