Harvey Keitel Movies, Ranked

Both National Treasures Beat Both Tarantinos

8 films, ranked on my own star ratings by Arwin Edward Bagaslao. Updated 2026-05-15.

Ranked with my own stars, and I already know this one is going to start arguments. The Last Temptation of Christ is my five, then both National Treasure films land above Pulp Fiction and Reservoir Dogs, and The Grand Budapest Hotel is parke

  1. 1
    The Last Temptation of Christ (1988)
    ★★★★★

    I don’t know why, but I felt really unmotivated to watch this.

  2. 2
    National Treasure (2004)
    ★★★★½
  3. 3
    National Treasure: Book of Secrets (2007)
    ★★★★½
  4. 4
    Taxi Driver (1976)
    ★★★

    Ang bata ni Robert De Niro dito. Parang batang Jake Gyllenhaal energy sa Donnie Darko.

  5. 5
    Pulp Fiction (1994)
    ★★★

    Pulp Fiction leans heavily into dialogue driven storytelling, prioritizing long conversations and character interactions over traditional action or spectacle.

  6. 6
    The Irishman (2019)
    ★½

    It has the same vibes as Goodfellas, The Godfather, and The Departed with its mafia centered story and criminal world.

  7. 7
    Reservoir Dogs (1992)
    ★½

    It felt like Among Us, nobody knows who the rat is and paranoia slowly poisons the room.

  8. 8
    The Grand Budapest Hotel (2014)
    ★

    Maganda ang visuals. The symmetry and color palette are very deliberate, almost storybook-like.
