Daniel Craig Movies, Ranked

Suits, Bruises, and Detective Sweat

11 films, ranked on my own star ratings by Arwin Edward Bagaslao. Updated 2026-09-22.

A Daniel Craig ranking from my watched films: Bond bruises, detective sweating, dry comedy, spy fatigue, and men who look like saving the world ruined their entire week. Ranked by stars first, then by tension, action clarity, rewatch value,

  1. 1
    Spectre (2015)
    ★★★★½

    This film looks gorgeous. Hoyte van Hoytema really proves himself as a worthy successor to Roger Deakins.

  2. 2
    Casino Royale (2006)
    ★★★★½

    A grounded, physical Bond that leans on tension and clean action rather than gadget fantasy (thank God!).

  3. 3
    Skyfall (2012)
    ★★★★

    Now this is a proper opening! Controlled and allowing the action to breathe, unlike the opening of Quantum of Solace with its super shaky camera movements and fast cuts.

  4. 4
    Lara Croft: Tomb Raider (2001)
    ★★★★
  5. 5
    Wake Up Dead Man (2025)
    ★★★★

    Nakakatawa agad yung confession scene. May dark humor agad.

  6. 6
    Glass Onion (2022)
    ★★★½
  7. 7
    Knives Out (2019)
    ★★★½
  8. 8
    Cowboys & Aliens (2011)
    ★★★

    I like this most when it leans fully into its weird cowboy and sci-fi blend.

  9. 9
    No Time to Die (2021)
    ★★

    Honestly, they should have ended the Craig Bond series with Spectre so it could have ended on a higher note.

  10. 10
    Quantum of Solace (2008)
    ★

    The film starts with chaotic camera movement and very fast cutting.

  11. 11
    Layer Cake (2004)
    ½

    Probably Matthew Vaughn’s most boring creation for me.
