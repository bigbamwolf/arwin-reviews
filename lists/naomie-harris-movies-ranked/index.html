Naomie Harris Movies, Ranked

Rage Virus, Moneypenny, Moonlight

11 films, ranked on my own star ratings by Arwin Edward Bagaslao. Updated 2026-08-29.

She outran the infected in 28 Days Later, handed Bond his gear across three films, then quietly wrecked me in Moonlight. Ranked by my own stars, and the distance between Rampage and Moonlight is the whole reason this list exists. ...plus 1

    Spectre (2015)
    ★★★★½

    This film looks gorgeous. Hoyte van Hoytema really proves himself as a worthy successor to Roger Deakins.

    ★★½

    Miami Vice feels like Michael Mann operating at a much larger scale, with more locations, moving parts, and glossy production value than his earlier work.

  9. 9
    Moonlight (2016)
    ★★½

    Moonlight is a coming of age story about a little black kid who grows up bullied by his own community, neglected by his junkie mom, and confused about his sexuality.
