James Bond Movies, Ranked

12 films, ranked on my own star ratings by Arwin Edward Bagaslao. Updated 2026-09-30.

James Bond is one of those franchises I can enjoy for very different reasons depending on the era, the actor, and the style of the film. Sometimes I am there for the cool factor, the action, and the spy fun. Other times I connect more with

  1. 1
    Spectre (2015)
    ★★★★½

    This film looks gorgeous. Hoyte van Hoytema really proves himself as a worthy successor to Roger Deakins.

  2. 2
    Casino Royale (2006)
    ★★★★½

    A grounded, physical Bond that leans on tension and clean action rather than gadget fantasy (thank God!).

  3. 3
    From Russia with Love (1963)
  4. 4
    Skyfall (2012)
    ★★★★

    Now this is a proper opening! Controlled and allowing the action to breathe, unlike the opening of Quantum of Solace with its super shaky camera movements and fast cuts.

  5. 5
    The Spy Who Loved Me (1977)
    ★★★★

    To be frank, there are some really nice cinematic shots here, especially in the desert sequences.

  6. 6
    Goldfinger (1964)
    ★★★

    Sean Connery really carried this film. His presence alone makes everything feel more engaging.

  7. 7
    No Time to Die (2021)
    ★★

    Honestly, they should have ended the Craig Bond series with Spectre so it could have ended on a higher note.

  8. 8
    Dr. No (1962)
    ★★

    Since this is the first James Bond film, I feel like they were still finding their footing and figuring out two things: what the overall tone and identity of Bond should…

  9. 9
    Moonraker (1979)
    ★½

    Fun action ideas and ambitious production design, but the tension never fully lands.

  10. 10
    GoldenEye (1995)
    ★½

    It feels like a mix of old school filmmaking transitioning into a more modern style.

  11. 11
    Quantum of Solace (2008)
    ★

    The film starts with chaotic camera movement and very fast cutting.
