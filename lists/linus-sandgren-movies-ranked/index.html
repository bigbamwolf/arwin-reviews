Linus Sandgren Movies, Ranked

9 films, ranked on my own star ratings by Arwin Edward Bagaslao. Updated 2026-05-02.

Linus Sandgren is one of those cinematographers whose work can feel elegant, alive, and easy to lock into once the images start flowing. There is usually a strong sense of movement, beauty, and visual rhythm in the films he shoots, but this

  1. 1
    La La Land (2016)
    ★★★★½

    A visually intoxicating love story about ambition, timing, and the painful trade off between dreams and relationships.

  2. 2
    Jay Kelly (2025)
    ★★★★½

    Jay Kelly is a quiet, reflective character study about success, isolation, and the cost of living at a level far removed from ordinary life.

  3. 3
    First Man (2018)
    ★★★★½

    Feels like Interstellar with Chernobyl level anxiety.

  4. 4
    Babylon (2022)
    ★★★★

    Nellie (Margot Robbie) and Manny’s (Diego Calva) conversation early on is immediately relatable.

  5. 5
    Wuthering Heights (2026)
    ★★★★

    I experience this as a tragic love story where the music and visuals pull me into a romance that feels beautiful, obsessive, and doomed.

  6. 6
    Don't Look Up (2021)
    ★★★★

    First few minutes pa lang, pinakita na agad ni Leonardo yung terrified look niya.

  7. 7
    Joy (2015)
    ★★★★

    This is a grounded startup story that takes its time building empathy for an underdog boxed in by financial pressure, family dynamics, and self doubt.

  8. 8
    No Time to Die (2021)
    ★★

    Honestly, they should have ended the Craig Bond series with Spectre so it could have ended on a higher note.

  9. 9
    Saltburn (2023)
    ★½

    The cinematography does a huge amount of heavy lifting for me here.
