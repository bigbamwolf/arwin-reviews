Holt McCallany Movies, Ranked

Broad Shoulders in Everyone Else's Movie

10 films, ranked on my own star ratings by Arwin Edward Bagaslao. Updated 2026-08-17.

I never went looking for this guy, he just kept turning up in films I already loved, from the fight club basement to the Von Erich gym. Ranked by my own stars, which means Sully edges out a movie I have rewatched way too many times.

  1. 1
    Sully (2016)
    ★★★★½

    A delay is better than a disaster. I don’t think I’ve ever gotten emotional just by hearing the words “one hundred fifty-five.” It’s like a courtroom drama.

  2. 2
    The Iron Claw (2023)
    ★★★★½
