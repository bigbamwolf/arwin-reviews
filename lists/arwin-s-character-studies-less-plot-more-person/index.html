Arwin's Character Studies: Less Plot, More Person

13 films, ranked on my own star ratings by Arwin Edward Bagaslao. Updated 2026-10-26.

Films where the real story is the person, not the plot. Addiction, obsession, ambition, wealth, grief, and the slow ways people break or settle. Every film here is one I called a character study in my own review. Ranked by my rating, highes

  1. 1
    Bugonia (2025)
    ★★★★★

    Much stronger on a second viewing because the film shifts from feeling like a mystery to feeling like an interpretation exercise.

  2. 2
    A Star Is Born (2018)
    ★★★★★

    A deeply emotional rise and fall romance about love, fame, and self destruction.

  3. 3
    The Green Knight (2021)
    ★★★★★

    A24’s Lord of the Rings, where the hardest knight to defeat is the coward hiding inside yourself.

  4. 4
    Quezon (2025)
    ★★★★½

    To be honest, di talaga ako fan ng history pag binabasa o pinapanood sa documentary form.

  5. 5
    Jay Kelly (2025)
    ★★★★½

    Jay Kelly is a quiet, reflective character study about success, isolation, and the cost of living at a level far removed from ordinary life.

  6. 6
    Uncut Gems (2019)
    ★★★★½

    Sa simula pa lang, may kadiri na agad. Ramdam ko na yung tension agad from the opening moments.

  7. 7
    Lady Vengeance (2005)
    ★★★★½

    The slow deliberate pacing kept me distant at first, but the second half pulled me in once the visuals became more precise and the colors drained away.

  8. 8
    A House of Dynamite (2025)
    ★★★½

    Parang The Office ang camera work: mockumentary style cinematography na may handheld shots, crash zooms, at sudden pans.

  9. 9
    Night Always Comes (2025)
    ★★★½

    Her mom is a terrible mom. Inuna pa yung sasakyan kaysa pamilya.

  10. 10
    Magazine Dreams (2023)
    ★★★½

    The film opens with a slow motion stage posing scene.

  11. 11
    All the Money in the World (2017)
    ★★★½

    What stayed with me most was how polished and controlled everything felt while the situation itself spiraled into chaos.

  12. 12
    Rocky (1976)
    ★★★

    Rocky feels less like a boxing movie and more like a character study about a guy who has spent his whole life settling.

  13. 13
    Ballad of a Small Player (2025)
    ★★½

    Set design is colorful since Macau ang depicted setting, may pagka Wes Anderson yung vibe ng interiors.
