Stanley Kubrick Movies, Ranked

8 films, ranked on my own star ratings by Arwin Edward Bagaslao. Updated 2026-09-04.

Stanley Kubrick is one of those directors I can admire even when a film does not fully connect with me. His movies feel controlled, exact, and unforgettable in their own way, whether I end up loving them or feeling pushed away by them. Ther

  1. 1
    Dr. Strangelove or: How I Learned to Stop Worrying and Love the Bomb (1964)
    ★★★★½

    What unsettles me most is how calmly it presents something so catastrophic, as if total destruction can be triggered by routine, ego, and blind protocol.

  2. 2
    Full Metal Jacket (1987)
    ★★★★

    Starts darkly funny then slowly turns into a cold, unsettling war experience.

  3. 3
    2001: A Space Odyssey (1968)
    ★★★★

    A meditative sci-fi experience that feels more like art than traditional storytelling.

  4. 4
    Paths of Glory (1957)
    ★★★½

    The scale of no man’s land is great, but the sound feels blown out and harsh at times.

  5. 5
    The Killing (1956)
    ★★★

    Citizen Kane’s fragmented, perspective driven storytelling meets Ocean’s Eleven’s meticulously planned ensemble heist.

  6. 6
    Eyes Wide Shut (1999)
    ★½

    I went in expecting something seductive and wild, but what I experienced was more of a psychological ego collapse than an erotic story.

  7. 7
    The Shining (1980)
    ★½

    I actually watched this before back in college because I was reading the book and wanted some visuals in my head.

  8. 8
    A Clockwork Orange (1971)
    ★½

    The film throws you into a world of delinquent energy and never lets you settle.
