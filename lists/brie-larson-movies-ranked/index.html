Brie Larson Movies, Ranked

An Oscar, Two Marvels, and a Giant Ape

8 films, ranked on my own star ratings by Arwin Edward Bagaslao. Updated 2026-09-18.

Room wrecked me and nothing else on this list gets close, which is exactly why the rest of the order looks the way it does. Ranked by my own stars, capes, Kong, and Mario all accounted for.

  1. 1
    Room (2015)
    ★★★★½

    Their room feels so cramped. I was under the impression that they were under experiment or that the world was already apocalyptic.

  2. 2
    Kong: Skull Island (2017)

  3. 3

    ★★★★½
  4. 4

    ★★★★

    Reminds me of The Green Mile (1999) with the trope of trying to bring justice to someone wrongly lined up for death row.

  5. 5
    The Super Mario Galaxy Movie (2026)

    Surprisingly good! I had no expectations going in, but wow just wow.
