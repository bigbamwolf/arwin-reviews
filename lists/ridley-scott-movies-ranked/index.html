Ridley Scott Movies, Ranked

17 films, ranked on my own star ratings by Arwin Edward Bagaslao. Updated 2026-10-11.

Ridley Scott films feel like worlds I can step into and get lost in. From ancient battles to future ruins, his movies are built on scale, atmosphere, and obsession with power, belief, and survival. This ranking is based on immersion and amb

  1. 1
    American Gangster (2007)
    ★★★★½

    Nothing flashy. Nothing gimmicky. Just good old fashioned storytelling by Ridley Scott.

  2. 2
