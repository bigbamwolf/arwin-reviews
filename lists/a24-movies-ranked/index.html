A24 Movies, Ranked

54 films, ranked on my own star ratings by Arwin Edward Bagaslao. Updated 2026-10-03.

A curated list of movies and shows that either come from A24. Emotional weight, visual identity, grounded performances, psychological depth, and that unmistakable “arthouse-but-accessible” vibe. This collection mixes certified A24 originals
