I Already Know How It Ends, Ranked

Somehow That Makes It Worse

16 films, ranked on my own star ratings by Arwin Edward Bagaslao. Updated 2026-08-15.

Films with zero mystery about the outcome, where the whole structure is the walk toward a thing I already grieved before the lights went down. ...plus 6 more. View the full list on Letterboxd .

  1. 1
    Pearl Harbor (2001)
    ★★★★★

    Big, emotional, and unapologetically cinematic.

  2. 2
    The Impossible (2012)
    ★★★★★
  3. 3
    The Voice of Hind Rajab (2025)
    ★★★★★

    I felt emotionally wrecked early on and never really recovered.

  4. 4
    Schindler's List (1993)
    ★★★★★

    On my first watch, I thought this was just about a capitalist exploiting people through his own version of forced labor.

  5. 5
    Sully (2016)
    ★★★★½

    A delay is better than a disaster. I don’t think I’ve ever gotten emotional just by hearing the words “one hundred fifty-five.” It’s like a courtroom drama.

  6. 6
    Argo (2012)
    ★★★★½

    What pulls me in most is the concept itself.

  7. 7
    First Man (2018)
    ★★★★½

    Feels like Interstellar with Chernobyl level anxiety.

  8. 8
    Chernobyl (2019)
    ★★★★½
  9. 9
    Only the Brave (2017)
    ★★★★½
  10. 10
    Polytechnique (2009)
    ★★★★½

    A Denis Villeneuve film where I can really feel the tension through the characters’ motivations and the musical score.

  11. 11
    All Quiet on the Western Front (2022)
    ★★★★½

    Spoiler Alert! The opening sequence feels like it was painted by Bob Ross himself: serene, beautiful, almost peaceful.

  12. 12
    The Lost Bus (2025)
    ★★★★½

    Sa unang tingin, akala ko isa lang itong typical disaster survival film: sunog, takbuhan, sigawan, tapos iyakan sa dulo.

  13. 13
    Heneral Luna (2015)
    ★★★★½
  14. 14
    Goyo: The Boy General (2018)
    ★★★★½
  15. 15
    Quezon (2025)
    ★★★★½

    To be honest, di talaga ako fan ng history pag binabasa o pinapanood sa documentary form.

  16. 16
    Warfare (2025)
    ★★★★½

    Tahimik ang simula, pero punong puno ng tensyon.
