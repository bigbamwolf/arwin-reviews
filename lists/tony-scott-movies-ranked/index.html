Tony Scott Movies, Ranked

Denzel Five Times, Throttle Wide Open

8 films, ranked on my own star ratings by Arwin Edward Bagaslao. Updated 2026-09-29.

Five of these eight star Denzel Washington, which was never a plan, it just turns out that is the pairing I keep going back to. Ranked by my own stars, and Top Gun landing this low on a Tony Scott list is going to cost me a few friendships.

  1. 1
    Enemy of the State (1998)
    ★★★★½
  2. 2
    Unstoppable (2010)
    ★★★★½

    I don’t think I have seen any film like this, with such a believable, imminent, monstrous train as if it is the main villain character of the story.

  3. 3
    Man on Fire (2004)
    ★★★★½
  4. 4
    Déjà Vu (2006)
    ★★★★½
  5. 5
    Crimson Tide (1995)
    ★★★★½

    This is basically Tony Scott’s Dr. Strangelove (1964).

  6. 6
    The Taking of Pelham 1 2 3 (2009)
    ★★★½

    You gotta trust a movie when it has a Scott directing, a train, and Denzel Washington right in the middle of it.

  7. 7
    Top Gun (1986)
    ★★★
  8. 8
    Days of Thunder (1990)
    ★★★

    Feels like Top Gun on asphalt. The film treats racing not just as a sport but as identity.
