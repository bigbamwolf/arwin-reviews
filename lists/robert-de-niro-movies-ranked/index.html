Robert De Niro Movies, Ranked

Men Staring Through Moral Damage

16 films, ranked on my own star ratings by Arwin Edward Bagaslao. Updated 2026-09-22.

A Robert De Niro ranking from my watched films: crime gravity, old-school intensity, tired fathers, dangerous mentors, and men who look like they have not had peace since 1974. Ranked by stars first, then by emotional weight, rewatch value,

  1. 1
    Heat (1995)
    ★★★★★

    Cute ni Natalie Portman and ang galing agad niya umarte kahit bata pa siya dito.

  2. 2
    Limitless (2011)
    ★★★★½

    Limitless begins at the end, like it’s telling us: this isn’t a success story.

  3. 3
    The Fan (1996)
  4. 4
    Killers of the Flower Moon (2023)
    ★★★★

    Detective style mystery, may pagka Knives Out with the world vibe of Sherlock Holmes and the grit of Peaky Blinders.

  5. 5
    The Godfather Part II (1974)
    ★★★★

    The Godfather Part II shifts away from loud violence into quiet power plays, where conversations, looks, and decisions carry more danger than gunfire.

  6. 6
    Joy (2015)
    ★★★★

    This is a grounded startup story that takes its time building empathy for an underdog boxed in by financial pressure, family dynamics, and self doubt.

  7. 7
    Jackie Brown (1997)
    ★★★½

    I immediately felt Tarantino’s signature rhythm in how tension is built through conversations, framing, and quiet maneuvering instead of nonstop action.

  8. 8
    Shark Tale (2004)
    ★★★½
  9. 9
    Joker (2019)
    ★★★½
  10. 10
    The Family (2013)
    ★★★½
  11. 11
    The Intern (2015)
    ★★★½

    Such a fun, light movie. Sometimes I go into movies completely blind without reading the premise or even watching the trailer, and this was one of those times.

  12. 12
    Taxi Driver (1976)
    ★★★

    Ang bata ni Robert De Niro dito. Parang batang Jake Gyllenhaal energy sa Donnie Darko.

  13. 13
    Silver Linings Playbook (2012)
    ★★½
  14. 14
    Raging Bull (1980)
    ★★

    Opening credits pa lang, ang ganda na agad ng framing.

  15. 15
    GoodFellas (1990)
    ★½

    A crime rise and fall story told directly from the main character’s perspective.

  16. 16
    The Irishman (2019)
    ★½

    It has the same vibes as Goodfellas, The Godfather, and The Departed with its mafia centered story and criminal world.
