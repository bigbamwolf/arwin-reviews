What a studio gets

One screening. Four surfaces. Two days.

01

Guaranteed coverage within 48 hours of the screening.

A full Letterboxd review, an Instagram review card on @bigbamwolf, a Facebook post and a permanent page on arwinreviews.com. Every one tags the studio or the distributor.

02

Tagged on every post

The studio or distributor handle rides on all four surfaces, so the coverage lands in your mentions, ready to reshare.

03

A quotable pull line

On request, one clean line written to be lifted for a poster, a social card or a press roundup.

04

Post screening recap

A short report after publishing with every live link and the reach each post earned.

05

Embargo respected

Nothing goes up before the lift time you set. The review is ready, it waits for your clock.

06

An honest rating

The same scale used on all 1,667 films logged. A strong score from this desk carries weight because it is earned.
