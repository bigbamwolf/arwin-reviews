# ARWIN REVIEWS outreach research list

Honest accounting: PH cinema marketing managers do not publish their direct emails. The contacts below are the **best publicly findable inboxes** for each org, plus the LinkedIn search query you run to find the named individual. The drafts I create will land at the public inbox first, with a polite ask to be forwarded to the right person if needed.

The fastest yes will come from distributors and festivals, not from the biggest cinema chains (those have layered approval).

## Realistic outcomes per category

- **Cinemas (1 to 6):** Slowest. Expect 1 to 2 replies out of 6, 3 to 6 weeks. Layered approvals.
- **Distributors (7 to 12):** Fastest. Pioneer Films and Solar are most likely to reply within a week, they actively buy press placements.
- **Festivals (13 to 16):** Press accreditation is almost guaranteed (free). The PAID partnership lane is a longer ask, depends on their year-end budget.

## Day 1 priority

If you only send 3 first, send to:
1. Pioneer Films (`info@pioneerfilms.ph`) — Template B, mention their next A24 release
2. Solar Pictures (`info@solarentertainment.com.ph`) — Template B, mention their next family/action release
3. QCinema (`qcinema.iff@gmail.com`) — Template C, ask for press accreditation + paid lane

These three have the highest open + reply rate.
