Quick answers

Can you guarantee a positive review?
No. The verdict reflects the film. Sponsorship buys the slot, the disclosure, the audience, and the production care, not the star count.
How is sponsored content disclosed?
A clearly labelled "Sponsored" or "Promoted" banner at the top of the post, an "in partnership with" line at the foot, and the brand tag in the URL slug. Same disclosure shows on social shares.
Do you do banner ads or pop-ups?
No banner ads, no pop-ups, no autoplay video. One promoted slot at the top of the home page when one is sold. Everything else is editorial.
What is the turnaround?
A sponsored opening-week review publishes within 72 hours of the screening, the week the film opens. Gear testing is a 14-day cycle. Brand partnerships are monthly.

Do you publish performance data?
Yes, every paid placement comes with a one-page recap: views, scroll depth, social reach, follow-on referrals. Sent within seven days of publish.
