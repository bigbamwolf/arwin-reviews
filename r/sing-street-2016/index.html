Sing Street 2016

★
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-03-25
Maybe I am just not that inclined to coming of age movies, that is why I did not really feel an emotional response here. But I still have to give it a star because I genuinely like the music (even if only for background noise). I also appreciate the bully’s character development. That one caught my attention and added a bit more depth to the story.
