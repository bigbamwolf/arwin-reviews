The Good Dinosaur 2015

★★★
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-04-25
The animation for the skin, mountains, and water is great. The environments look beautiful and textured, especially the landscapes and natural elements. Me and my daughter laughed so hard during the scene where they ate something and got high. That part was unexpectedly funny. The film is about courage, belonging, accepting loss, leaving your own mark, and finding your way back home. It is a simple story, but the themes are easy to connect with, especially when watching it with family.
