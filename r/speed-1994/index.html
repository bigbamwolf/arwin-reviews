Speed 1994

★★★
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-08-29
The acting, direction, camera work, and score are great. I really felt the danger and heroics. It is not as heart pounding as Unstoppable (2010), but still good enough. Real old school explosions! There is something so satisfying about seeing actual fireballs, debris, and practical destruction instead of everything made through CGI. Can I just say, Sandra Bullock looks so much better here?
