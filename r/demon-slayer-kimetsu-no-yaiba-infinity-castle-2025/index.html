Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba Infinity Castle 2025

★★★★
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2025-09-02
After 7 years balik otaku phase na ako 😅 Last anime ko pa was One Piece episode 716, 2018 pa yun! Grabe ang tagal. Tapos ngayon, first time ko pa manood ng anime movie sa sinehan. Had to do my assignment as in 63 episodes in one weekend. Honestly akala ko itong movie na ito would feel different like iba yung flow, pangcinema talaga. Pero it felt more like anime episodes lang na pinagdikit dikit to make it movie length. Mahahalata mo talaga yung mga “episode breaks” kahit nasa big screen ka na. Story wise wala pa rin akong sugoi moment. Sound design tho ibang level. Yumayanig yung IMAX. Ramdam mo bawat clash, bawat attack. Ang galing. Tapos yung musical score, panalo rin. May techno electro feels na bagay sa vibe ng Demon Slayer. Highlight for me: Zenitsu vs Kaigaku. Visual feast talaga. Thunder Breathing hits different on the big screen. Letdown? Akaza. Parang si Sentry lang defeated by the power of love. 😆 Medyo anti climactic pero sige na nga, may hugot. Overall di man super mind blowing, solid pa rin as a comeback experience sa anime world. Nakakamiss pala talaga tong ganito.🤺
