Armed Only with a Camera: The Life and Death of Brent Renaud 2025

★★★★½
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-03-15
Another heavy documentary about the effects of war and terrorism on ordinary people. It focuses on the human cost of conflict, which makes everything feel more personal. The film is well made and very direct with what it wants to show: the reality of what people (and journalists) endure in these situations. Super heavy to watch.
