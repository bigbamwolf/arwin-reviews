The Voice of Hind Rajab 2025

★★★★★
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-03-03
Non spoiler thoughts (summary): I felt emotionally wrecked early on and never really recovered. The film locks me into a confined space and refuses to let me breathe, using sound and stillness to make the helplessness unbearable. It feels less like watching a war story and more like being forced to listen to something that should never have happened. ⚠️ Spoilers ahead. From here on, plot details are discussed. Read on if you’ve seen the film. If not, save this post and come back after. ⚠️ Gripping war story told through the eyes of call center agents, with the real voice of a child trapped in a war zone. This is similar to The Call 2013 and The Guilty 2021 in structure, but this one is far more heart wrenching. This film is heavy. Less than 30 minutes in and I was already in tears. The film traps me inside that car with Hind Rajab, surrounded by her dead family while troops are firing in her direction. At the same time, I feel the frustration of Omar, Rana, and Mahdi whose hands are tied. Everyone is helpless in different ways. The child is physically trapped, while the adults are trapped by protocol, fear, and limitations. The actors are great. The camera work is great too. It feels like an actual observer inside the room. Some angles subtly show them losing emotional control. The cinematography chooses lenses that feel tight and claustrophobic, enough to trap me inside their heads. I also love the mirror and monitor reflections between characters. It almost looks like a cross fade or double exposure, suggesting how the situation is getting inside them psychologically. It really feels like Hind Rajab versus layers and layers of bureaucracy. Not just bullets, not just war, but paperwork, procedure, waiting, and helplessness. The director’s choice to blend real voice recordings and actual footage of Omar, Rana, and Nisreen adds painful realism. It blurs the line between film and reality. Hindi na siya parang movie lang. It feels like a document of something that should not have happened. By the end, I was not thinking about technique anymore. I was just thinking about how war reduces everything to numbers and protocols while a child is begging to survive. War sucks! And this film makes sure I feel that truth in my bones.
