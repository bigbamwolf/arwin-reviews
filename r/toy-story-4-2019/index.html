Toy Story 4 2019

★★
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-09-02
The cinematography is the highlight for me. There’s a huge difference compared with the first two films. It feels like they are using prime or longer lenses, with shallower depth of field and this really nice creamy bokeh in the background. that gives the movie a much more cinematic look. Weakest of the franchise for me, though. There are just a lot of new things happening, and it feels more crowded compared with the simplicity of the first two films. I miss some of the simplicity and tighter focus of the first two films.
