Past Lives (2023) review

★★★★½
Rated 4.5/5
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-01-22
Drama, Romance · Directed by Celine Song · 105 min
600 reviews · 1,526 films logged · 155,998 words
This review contains spoilers after the non spoiler section. Non spoiler thoughts (summary): A quiet, intimate film that leans heavily on silence, pauses, and unspoken emotion. Conversations feel mature and deeply human, especially in how reassurance, insecurity, and acceptance are handled. Gently paced, beautifully shot, and emotionally honest. Past Lives will hurt you in the most beautiful way. ⚠️ Spoilers ahead. From here on, plot details are discussed. Read on if you’ve seen the film. If not, save this post and come back after. ⚠️ Warm start. It immediately feels like a K-drama, soft and intimate, quietly pulling you in. There’s a sense of familiarity and comfort. There is still clearly an attraction between Nora and Hae Sung, even if Nora is already married. It’s not loud or explicit, but it lingers. You feel it in the pauses, in the way they look at each other, and in what they choose not to say. The silence, the stares, the stillness. They all carry an invisible weight. There are so many unsaid feelings and unspoken regrets. I really loved Nora and her husband Arthur’s long bed conversation. Arthur opening up about his insecurities and Nora reassuring him that they are the ones meant for each other. Hindi lang babae ang may kailangan ng reassurance. Even men need it too. That scene alone adds so much emotional maturity to the film. The conversation between Nora and Hae Sung, on the other hand, is painful. It’s all about the one who got away and the weight of all the “what ifs.” Just a quiet acceptance mixed with regret. The score is also nice throughout the film, subtle but emotionally effective. Beautifully shot as well, especially during the most intense moments where the camera just lets the actors exist in the frame without forcing anything. This film hurts you gently, patiently, and honestly. Past Lives will hurt you in the most beautiful way.
