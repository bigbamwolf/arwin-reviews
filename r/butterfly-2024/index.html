Butterfly 2024

★★★
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-03-10
Ang galing nung parang oil painting technique. It really feels like watching a moving canvas. Every frame looks like a painting slowly coming to life. I think this is the first time I have watched an animation done in this style, and it immediately caught my attention. I also like the match cuts between memories and the present. The transitions are very smooth. Scenes from the past blend naturally into the current moment, which makes the storytelling feel fluid. What stays with me most is how the film uses warmth and nostalgia to draw me in before revealing a much heavier emotional weight underneath. For me, it works as both an artistic showcase and an emotional experience.
