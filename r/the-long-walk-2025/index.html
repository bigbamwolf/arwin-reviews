The Long Walk 2025

★★★★½
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-01-03
Menacing Mark Hamill as always. He brings that intimidating authority, ramdam mo agad yung pressure. Nice to see familiar faces too: Charlie Plummer from Looking for Alaska, na mabait doon pero dito kupal, which works really well for the dynamic. David Jonsson from Alien Romulus also stands out here, mas maraming lines ngayon and the same quality of acting. He feels more confident and grounded, hindi lang filler character. It is another contest game to remove oneself from poverty, like The Running Man and The Hunger Games, but what sets this apart is that it has a lot of heart. Even though they are competitors, they genuinely care for each other. Hindi lang survival for the prize, survival din of their humanity. You feel that they are walking not just for themselves, but sometimes for the people beside them. This is more about cardiovascular and muscular endurance mixed with mental toughness, because it is nonstop walking. Bawal magpahinga. Intense yung uphill, mentally and physically exhausting, and you can really feel the fatigue building up as the miles pile on. Hindi ito tungkol sa bilis or strength alone, kundi sa kung sino yung kayang hindi bumigay. Kudos to the actors. Ang hirap ng continuous shots habang naglalakad, umaarte, at nagdedeliver ng mahahabang lines nang sabay sabay. Hindi lang physical acting, kundi emotional endurance din. The only thing I kept wondering is how they even remember each other’s names in such a short period of time of getting to know each other, especially under that kind of stress. Ganda ng shot sa mile 201. Silhouette plus backlit shot, very striking and symbolic. Maganda rin sa mile 260, parang sunset landscape sa Sinners, may sense of beauty kahit nasa gitna ng exhaustion at hopelessness. Ganda ng twist sa bastard ni Major. It reframes his presence and makes his role more meaningful. The last walk was dramatic and cinematic. Raining, yellow brown color, and vengeance served. Ramdam mo na hindi na lang ito tungkol sa pagkapanalo, kundi about closure. Ang rich ng backstory ng bawat isa, and lahat may malalim na dahilan bakit sila lumalaban. Hindi sila basta contestants. They are people running away from something, or walking toward something they desperately need.
