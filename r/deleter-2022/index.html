Deleter (2022) review

★★
Rated 2/5
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-10-29
626 reviews · 1,552 films logged · 162,974 words
This movie made me want to delete it myself. I always wonder what this kind of people’s job is like. It’s so fucked up, especially in this cruel, evil world, and this shows a glimpse of it. Imagine going to work every day and your actual job is to absorb the worst things humanity uploads so the rest of us don’t have to see them. I also appreciate seeing a Filipino horror film tackle such a strange and very modern kind of work. Content moderation is something almost everyone benefits from but barely anyone thinks about the people actually doing it. I don’t know, but at the back of my mind, is this a satire of being a corporate slave? A metaphor? It’s a funny thought even if it is not the intention of the director. Or maybe it is a social commentary on the ones who are wide awake but don’t do anything? Those who just watch injustice and remain silent? I actually like this interpretation more. If yes, I like that better than the forced psychological horror about guilt and trauma because it is not scary or anything. The horror side never really gave me the fear or tension I wanted. But at least the cinematography is nice. I like the dark office spaces, the monitor glow, and how cold and lifeless the workplace feels. The visuals sell the uneasiness better than the actual scares do. I think the premise and social commentary are more interesting than the actual horror. There are some good ideas and nice cinematography here, but the psychological horror never really works for me. Nothing particularly stands out either, which makes the whole experience feel underwhelming. Verdict: Weak Watched on Prime
