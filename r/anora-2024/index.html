Anora 2024

★★★★½
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-05-03
This feels like a mix of The Wolf of Wall Street, The Hangover, and Project X. It has that chaotic party energy, wild decisions, and people chasing pleasure without thinking too much about the consequences. The shots are beautiful. The lighting and grading are also good, giving the film a textured look. The first hour feels like Fifty Shades of Grey because of its erotic side and fantasy driven setup. But after that, the film shifts into something closer to Good Time, Uncut Gems, or Marty Supreme. It becomes one problem after another. Mikey Madison demonstrated a great performance and Vache Tovmasyan is hella funny bro!
