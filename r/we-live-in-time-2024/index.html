We Live in Time 2024

★★
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-05-04
Great acting by Andrew and Florence. I am with Florence’s character here, Almut. Not gonna lie, I have these thoughts of the choices I would make if I had cancer. I am also afraid of passing from this world into oblivion, leaving this planet without some imprint that people can remember me by. This film becomes more about legacy, time, and how we choose to live knowing that everything is temporary. The film could be better if it was narrated in a linear storytelling. The non linear structure takes me away from the emotional build up that could have been stronger if the story unfolded more directly.
