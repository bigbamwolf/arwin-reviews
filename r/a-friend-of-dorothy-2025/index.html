A Friend of Dorothy 2025

★★★
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-03-16
The vibes reminded me a lot of The Intouchables. Both films focus on an unlikely friendship where two very different people slowly open up to each other. In The Intouchables it was the bond between a wealthy disabled man and his caretaker. Here it carries that same warmth where companionship grows from understanding rather than obligation. In the end both films feel like feel good stories, and they are touching.
