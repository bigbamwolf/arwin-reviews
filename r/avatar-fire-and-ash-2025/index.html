Avatar: Fire and Ash 2025

★★★
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2025-12-17
So ngayon may suicide bomber na rin. That alone already changes the tone of the franchise. The film explores heavier themes like grief, seclusion, guilt, and even suicide. Mas dark siya emotionally compared to the earlier films. There is also an exploration of satanistic or pagan elements, tied to abandonment of faith. It feels like the story is intentionally pushing Avatar into darker territory. Visually, Cameron still mastered water VFX. Not just the sea and ocean like before, but also rivers and swamps. Technically impressive pa rin when it comes to environmental work. The lore deepens a bit with Spider now being able to breathe without a mask. That detail stood out and hints at bigger biological or spiritual changes in this world. I was kind of hoping for real character development for Miles Quaritch played by Stephen Lang, but none. Same old vengeance toward Jake. No evolution. Missed opportunity. Varang played by Oona Chaplin is great. Her performance and facial capture are very effective. Very menacing presence. She feels like the strongest new element of the film. That said, there is no new visual tech that made me go wow. It did not feel as immersive as before. It also felt long. Two hours and thirty minutes but still boring in parts. The pacing goes up and down and never really stabilizes. A lot of it feels recycled. Parang pinaghalo lang yung Avatar 1 and Way of Water, then presented as a new chapter. Even some shots feel reused. Wala masyadong bagong visual language. Sound design is also not immersive. I expected more, especially from a franchise known for technical excellence. There is also this feeling of Gen Z talking to Gen X. Ayaw makinig sa reason ng mga elders, especially Tulkun related decisions. The conflict feels generational but not always well handled. Parang hindi rin naka 48 fps tulad ng dati, or at least it did not feel as smooth or impactful as before. Overall, it feels like more of the same. It honestly feels like a cash grab. At this point, gusto ko na tawagin ang pamilya ni Jake na “Sullies ampunan” dahil tatlo na ang ampon nila. And Eywa was a letdown. Yun na yun? Three movies of hype and buildup, tapos ganun lang ang payoff. In the end, it failed to surpass Way of Water.
