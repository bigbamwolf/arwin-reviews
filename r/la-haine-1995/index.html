La Haine 1995

★½
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-03-30
Bunch of youths with nothing to do but disrupt peace. Dude just get a job! It reminded me of those characters in Netflix’s Steve, that same restless and directionless energy. Honestly I do not enjoy watching these kinds of delinquents. But I have to admit, it started to get my attention when they went to Astérix’s apartment. That stretch felt more focused and interesting compared to the earlier parts. Vinz’s actor is really great though. Nakakainis talaga yung mukha niya. Even in other films he carries that same energy. You just want to punch him, which means he is doing something right.
