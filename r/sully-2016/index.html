Sully 2016

★★★★½
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-08-15
A delay is better than a disaster. I don’t think I’ve ever gotten emotional just by hearing the words “one hundred fifty-five.” It’s like a courtroom drama. Even without an actual courtroom, every testimony, simulation, and question feels like a trial. Everyone (including me) is holding their breath while Sully’s judgment is being dissected second by second. I put this off so many times, and I’m glad I finally gave it a chance. Solid performance from Tom Hanks. Solid direction from Clint Eastwood. I think I really need to watch more Clint Eastwood films.
