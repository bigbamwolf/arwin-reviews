The Out-Laws 2023

★★★½
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-04-11
Fun heist and a funny B movie. I laughed so hard. This is one of those just turn off your brain and enjoy kind of films. The graveyard set and the action sequence there are actually nice. It adds a bit of style to the chaos and makes that part stand out.
