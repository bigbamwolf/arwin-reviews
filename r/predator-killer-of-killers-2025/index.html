Predator: Killer of Killers (2025) review

★★★★
Rated 4/5
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-08-31
Animation, Action, Adventure · Directed by Dan Trachtenberg, Joshua Wassung · 85 min
607 reviews · 1,533 films logged · 157,261 words
I love the idea that the Predators have this culture of hunting down the best of the best instead of just targeting random human beings as prey. It makes the hunt feel more like a test of strength and worthiness. The first story feels like Vikings, with that raw, brutal warrior energy. The second shifts into a samurai era vibe, more disciplined and honor driven. Then the third suddenly feels like Top Gun, with the aerial combat and high speed military action giving it a completely different flavor. Together, they fought valiantly and bravely. I really hope this eventually gets a live action adaptation because the concept has so much potential. The post credit scene is sick!
