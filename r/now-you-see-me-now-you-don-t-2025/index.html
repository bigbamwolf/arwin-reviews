Now You See Me: Now You Don't 2025

★★★★
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2025-11-23
Fun heist with a score that sometimes feels like Mission Impossible. Light, quick, engaging. Weird accent choices for Rosamund Pike as Veronica Vanderberg, parang pilit na pilit yung German accent. Feels like a reunion after 10 long years! Nakakatuwa makita ulit silang lahat in one frame. Nandun pa rin yung elements of old school treasure hunt vibe with hidden doors, puzzle clues, secret mechanisms. Parang classic adventure mixed with modern showmanship. Daming mirrors! Kawawa mga VFX artists dito hahaha. Ang hirap magtrack ng reflections while digitally erasing cameras in the reflections. Nice twist ulit as always. And it really feels like a soft reboot or passing of the torch. Entertaining and fun to watch. Solid trilogy.
