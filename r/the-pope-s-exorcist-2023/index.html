The Pope's Exorcist 2023

★★½
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-04-01
The boy’s acting is great. He really sells the possession. The sets are also incredible, lalo na yung hidden underground. That part adds a lot to the atmosphere. It feels ancient, secretive, and dangerous. I also like the idea that a possessed priest was the one influencing the queen to carry out the Inquisition. That concept is actually brilliant. It adds a layer of corruption from within the church itself. It reminded me of Seklusyon, where evil infiltrates the church to do its bidding from the inside. <blockquote>Jesus of Nazareth commands you. And now, I adjure you in His name!</blockquote>
