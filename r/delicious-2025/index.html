Delicious 2025

★★½
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2025-12-29
Magaling mang manipulate si Teodora. Ramdam mo kung gaano siya ka calculated at ka smooth sa pag control ng situation. Dahil dito, parang ayaw ko na tuloy maghire ng kasambahay. Unfortunately, hindi masyadong nag work yung payoff for me. Since hindi naman masyadong pinakita yung cannibalism, parang kulang yung impact. Nahulaan ko na rin siya sa simula pa lang, so walang masyadong shock or tension pagdating sa reveal. Mas prefer ko yung Fresh (2022). Interesting setup, strong manipulation angle, but the execution at the end didn’t fully deliver.
