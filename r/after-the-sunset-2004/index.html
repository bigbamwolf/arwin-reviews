After the Sunset 2004

★★
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-08-29
Not quite a fast-paced heist movie. It is more of a character struggle about a thief caught between two lives: continuing to do the one thing he knows he is exceptionally good at, or finally walking away from it to build a peaceful life with the woman he loves. The last act is full of deception and people constantly trying to outsmart one another. Everyone trying to stay one step ahead and making me question who is actually playing whom. Salma and Pierce are such a hot couple.
