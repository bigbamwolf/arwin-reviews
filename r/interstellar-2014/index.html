Interstellar (2014) review

★★★★★
Rated 5/5
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2025-01-15
621 reviews · 1,547 films logged · 160,724 words
Natuparan din yung pangarap kong mapanood ang Interstellar sa IMAX. Grabe, ibang klase talaga yung feeling. The launch sequences alone make you feel like you’re in the cockpit yourself. Every vibration, every rumble, is heart pounding. Hans Zimmer’s musical score remains surreal, still echoing like a prayer across time. Kaya believable ang illusion ng scale because it’s not purely visual effects, it’s craftsmanship. Even the farm sequences ground the story in something tactile dahil they built an entire farm for the film! The ships were built, not simulated. Light and darkness serve as emotional language here. Darkness represents isolation, loss, and the vast unknown while light becomes memory, guidance, and faith. The tesseract sequence is the ultimate symbol of love transcending dimensions. Time literally folds so emotion can pass through. Love becomes the only force that ignores the laws of physics. It feels like a story between humanity’s instinct to solve everything, and the universe’s divine mystery that cannot be solved. As Interstellar is shown again in cinemas, my interest in cosmic wonders reignites particularly with relativity and multiple dimension planes. I learned that when someone is near a gravity dense environment, time moves differently. The stronger the gravity, the faster time passes compared to ours on Earth. If that’s true, maybe our Heavenly Father is in that kind of dimension. Dahil an hour for Him can be decades or centuries sa atin. So what does that mean? Hindi natin dapat isipin na mabagal Siya o nakakainip. He views time differently from us. His timing is perfect. We are the ones moving slowly, yet still on time in His plan. And then there’s the idea of higher dimensional beings. Those existing in 5D, who perceive time not as a line, but as a tangible structure. They can see all our past, present, and future at once. So don’t worry, God knows your past, your present, and your future. Worry no more because He is bigger than your problems. Know that everything is already written and finished. Interstellar in IMAX is not just a film. It’s an experience that reminds you how small you are, and yet how much you matter in the vastness of everything. Ten years later, it still feels ahead of its time (maybe because it exists outside time itself).
