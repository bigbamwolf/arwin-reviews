Ice Road: Vengeance 2025

★
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-08-24
There are actually some breathtaking shots, but I kept getting taken out of the experience whenever it switched to obvious studio green screen shots. The contrast is so noticeable that it pulls me out of scenes that should have felt much bigger and more dangerous. The action and sense of urgency never really grabbed my attention either. Everything just feels too shallow, from the action to the emotional investment, so the movie never really gave me a reason to care about what was happening.
