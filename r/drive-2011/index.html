Drive 2011

★★
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-02-15
I watched this after reading a trivia that this is the last film capturing the yellow LA streetlights before they were replaced by LED lights that emit white light. This feels like Baby Driver with the treatment of Taxi Driver and Collateral visually. The cinematography and color grading are beautiful. It is a beautifully shot revenge film, but for me the story is just so so.
