Strange Frequencies: Taiwan Killer Hospital 2024

½
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-09-05
Basically, vloggers doing stupid things inside a horror movie. The characters are also built around very familiar stereotypes. Alexa is the dumb girl of the group who already has issues with everyone else. There’s the scaredy cat gay character, the producer who keeps forcing everyone to push forward because they need to accomplish their goal, the sickly dude who is already sensitive and vulnerable from the beginning, and the bully who acts overly brave and keeps pulling pranks. Then there’s the strong girl who seems unaffected by almost everything happening around her and remains the most logical thinker of the group. The "friendly fire" scene is one of the better moments. The prosthetics look good, and Jane’s acting helps sell the pain and panic.
