Wolfs 2024

★★½
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2025-11-21
Two clean up job artists forced to work together. Simple premise, no unnecessary complications. Ang ganda ng pasok ng Smooth Operator, sakto yung vibe, parang effortless cool moment na nagbigay ng konting personality sa film kahit medyo flat yung ibang parts. Maraming green and red lighting and grading choices. Noticeable siya, pero hindi naman deep or symbolic looking. Parang style choice lang to set the mood, not really something that changes the experience. Overall, wala namang spectacular na moments. Pwede siyang background noise habang may ginagawa ka. Entertaining enough to finish, pero hindi siya yung tipo na iiisipin mo after watching.
