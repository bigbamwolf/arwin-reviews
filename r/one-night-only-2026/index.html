One Night Only (2026) review

★★★½
Rated 3.5/5
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-08-28
613 reviews · 1,539 films logged · 158,229 words
The premise is pretty clever. It is basically The Purge (2013), but instead of legalized violence, imagine a world built around prohibiting premarital sex. And surprisingly, the execution of the movie is great. Feels like Music and Lyrics (2007) mixed with No Strings Attached (2011). It has that same light, cheesy, feel-good rom-com energy of Music and Lyrics, where two people slowly build a connection through constant banter, awkward situations, and genuinely sweet moments. Then it has the No Strings Attached side where sex and casual intimacy are treated playfully at first, but underneath all of that, the characters are really looking for something deeper and more meaningful. Both films also have that “love finds you when you’re not really looking for it” vibe. The relationship starts from a somewhat unconventional setup, but as they spend more time together, the jokes and physical attraction slowly turn into actual emotional attachment. It is cheesy, predictable, and romantic in the best possible way. There are many funny moments. Cheesy and romantic, especially with its theme of waiting for love and finding genuine connection, and realizing that intimacy means more when there is actually something emotional behind it.
