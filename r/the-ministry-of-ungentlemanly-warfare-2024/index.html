The Ministry of Ungentlemanly Warfare 2024

★★½
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-05-03
If you love stealthy unsanctioned missions that go boom when the time is right, you are in for a treat. This feels like Inglourious Basterds with its group of Nazi killers and ultra violent satisfaction. There is something absolutely satisfying about watching them take down Nazi soldiers one by one. The score also reminds me of Django Unchained, giving the whole thing a stylish and playful edge even during the violence. But this time, add lots of sailing, Churchill, secret operations, and spy agents.
