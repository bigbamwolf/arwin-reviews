American Sniper (2014) review

★★★★
Rated 4/5
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-08-15
620 reviews · 1,546 films logged · 160,269 words
Bradley’s eyes can really tell a thousand words. Essentially, American Sniper is about war PTSD and how difficult it is for soldiers to simply return to normal society after everything they have experienced. There is also this rage with finishing the job, until duty and family begin pulling him in opposite directions. It kinda sucks because he is considered a legend and a hero for what he does, yet at home, that same dedication becomes something his family has to suffer through. The final battle inside the sandstorm is pure tension. Visibility disappears, everything becomes chaotic. It is a great way to make the last battle feel suffocating and unpredictable.
