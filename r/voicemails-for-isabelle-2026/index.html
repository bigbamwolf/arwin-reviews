Voicemails for Isabelle (2026) review

★★★
Rated 3/5
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-06-27
601 reviews · 1,527 films logged · 156,111 words
Zoey Deutch's Jill looks like a mix of Cobie Smulders, Lily Collins, Daisy Ridley, and Natalie Portman. It is not just the facial resemblance. She has Cobie Smulders' effortless confidence and dry wit, Lily Collins' warmth and approachable charm, Daisy Ridley's youthful determination and emotional sincerity, and Natalie Portman's quiet intelligence and vulnerability. Those qualities blend together into a character who feels both familiar and uniquely her own. The film is packed with pop culture references, making the dialogue and interactions feel playful. The movie's themes blur between My Sister's Keeper (2009), Before Sunset (2004), and Chef (2014). carries the emotional weight of family and difficult choices, the intimate conversations and human connection of Before Sunset, and the healing, self discovery, and appreciation for life's simple moments found in Chef. The soundtrack is also really good. It complements the emotional beats and gives the film a warm, comforting vibe.
