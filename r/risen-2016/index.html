Risen (2016) review

★★★★
Rated 4/5
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-10-08
Action, Drama, History · Directed by Kevin Reynolds · 107 min
624 reviews · 1,550 films logged · 162,502 words
When dealing with the era of Jesus, the sets, locations, and costumes are very important for immersing me in the period. This one passed. It does not look like a cheap production at all. Very well made. I also like how the Romans become increasingly baffled and frustrated while persecuting Yeshua’s followers, while the followers themselves remain joyful because of how firm they are in their beliefs. Watched on Netflix.
