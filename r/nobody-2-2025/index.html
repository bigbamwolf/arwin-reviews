Nobody 2 (2025) review

★★½
Rated 2.5/5
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-08-09
Action, Comedy, Crime · Directed by Timo Tjahjanto · 89 min
626 reviews · 1,552 films logged · 162,974 words
Don’t bring a machete to a fucking katana duel. Still has those satisfying, ferocious, brutal fight scenes with cool camera work. The cinematography really shines in the final battle. It feels like they are inside a vibrant music video despite how violent the set piece is. Sharon Stone did a great job as the main villain. She has this commanding presence as a strong female antagonist who does not need some complicated sympathetic motivation. She is simply ruthless, powerful, and driven by pure evil intent
