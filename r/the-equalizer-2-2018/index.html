The Equalizer 2 2018

★★★★
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-08-08
The car fight scene where the assassin pretends to be Robert’s passenger is such a creative action setup. I have never seen that kind of action setup before. I also like it when Robert uses the environment to hide his location and pull off stealth moves. The kills are more brutal this time, and the story feels more personal. Robert is no longer simply dispensing justice for strangers. This time, the violence hits much closer to home.
