Back to the Future Part III (1990) review

★★★★
Rated 4/5
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-03-27
Adventure, Comedy, Sci-Fi · Directed by Robert Zemeckis · 118 min
607 reviews · 1,533 films logged · 157,261 words
Such a fun, adventurous, and lighthearted franchise. I really cannot get enough of it. And putting it in an old western setting was an incredible idea. The visuals alone already make it feel fresh again while still keeping the same charm. I like how this one gives more depth to Doc Emmett’s character. Seeing him in love adds another layer to him. Thomas F. Wilson as Buford Tannen is also great here. He completely disappears into the role. It still carries that same energy of fun, chaos, and clever storytelling. Great ending to a fantastic trilogy!
