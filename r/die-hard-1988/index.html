Die Hard (1988) review

★★★★★
Rated 5/5
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2025-12-24
600 reviews · 1,526 films logged · 155,998 words
Iba talaga ang charisma ni Bruce Willis nung bata bata pa. Ramdam mo agad yung presence niya. The entry of the bad guys is clean and sleek. Very confident. Alam mong calculated lahat. The rooftop action is insane. The danger feels real, and so does the acting. Survival siya, so dapat clumsy yung galaw. Hindi polished. And it works. Samahan mo pa ng solid musical score and the tension really builds. It honestly feels like Home Alone for adults. Same logic of a man using the environment to outsmart intruders, pero mas brutal at mas grounded. Makapigil hininga yung elevator shaft scene. Simple setup, pero sobrang effective. Tapos biglang familiar yung boses ni Hans. Sabi ko na e. Si Severus Snape pala. Alan Rickman. Bata bata pa siya dito kaya hindi ko agad namukhaan, pero the voice is unmistakable. Kada salita parang naririnig ko yung panlalait kay Harry Potter. He elevates the whole film effortlessly. Menacing without even trying. Ang galing! Nice real explosives. Sakto rin yung amount ng blood. Hindi excessive, pero ramdam mo yung stakes. The jump from the roof scene is absolute cinema. The leap itself plus the explosion behind him is exhilarating. Gets ko na ngayon why this became a cult classic. This is how you make a really good action film. Halos lahat shot in camera. Ramdam mo yung physicality. The standoff scene between John and Hans with his wife is beautifully shot. Silhouette ni John with sparks flying behind him. Freaking great visual. Parang yung mga ending ng musical fireworks display. I also like the emotional arc of Holly McClane. At the start, she distances herself from John by not using his surname. By the end, when she proudly uses “McClane” again, it feels earned. It is not just romantic, it is about reclaiming identity, partnership, and respect after surviving everything together. May character development din si Al Powell. That moment when he finally draws his gun and shoots, exactly when he is needed the most, feels earned. Perfect Christmas movie. I watched it December 24, 2025, and it still holds up after all these years.
