I Am Not Big Bird (2024) review

★½
Rated 1.5/5
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-11-17
665 reviews · 1,590 films logged · 180,255 words
The Naked Director meets 3 Idiots. Reminded me of Netflix's The Naked Director (2019), but this time with a bunch of morons like the group in 3 Idiots (2009). It has that same ridiculous world of the adult entertainment industry, with porn stars, producers, and shady people operating behind the scenes. But unlike The Naked Director, which focuses more on the business and rise of the Japanese porn industry, this one throws a bunch of clueless Filipino friends into that world through a case of mistaken identity. It also feels like a Filipino version of Paul Thomas Anderson's Boogie Nights (1997), but with the chaotic barkada energy of 3 Idiots. Both explore the absurd world of the adult entertainment industry, but while Boogie Nights takes its characters and their ambitions seriously, this one leans heavily into the stupidity of people who clearly have no idea what they're getting themselves into. The friendship and chaotic misadventures also reminded me of 3 Idiots, although that film has more heart and a stronger emotional payoff. Here, the characters keep finding themselves in increasingly ridiculous situations, and their questionable decisions only make everything worse. The comedy is not in the wittiness but in the simplicity of the stupidity. It has that very Filipino barkada humor where everyone thinks they know what they are doing, but nobody actually does. I like the concept and the ridiculous Filipino barkada humor, but I honestly expected to laugh much more. I only chuckled a few times, and I found myself frequently waiting for something genuinely funny to happen. Even the ending didn't deliver the payoff I was hoping for. Verdict: Bad, but with one or two redeeming elements Watched on Netflix
