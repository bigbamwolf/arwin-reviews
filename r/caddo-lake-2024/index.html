Caddo Lake 2024

★★★½
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-04-28
The family drama and characters were not that interesting for me at first, but everything makes sense as the story unfolds. There are some bittersweet afterthoughts that stayed with me. Those realizations give the story more emotional weight after everything clicks. I get why it was recommended after watching Dark. Definitely worth a rewatch.
