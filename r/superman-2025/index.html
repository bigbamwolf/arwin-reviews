Superman 2025

★★★
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2025-07-09
Right now, I guess I am one of the snyderbots. Missed Snyder's cinematography style. This new Superman film really gives the James Gunn DNA. Feels like a crossover style between GOTG and 2021 Suicide Squad. Lex feels like invulnerable. He's like playing Injustice with the combos. 😅 But the way Superman defeated him is similar how they defeated Ayesha sa GOTG 2 and kinda similar din sa Ready Player One. Bold statement yung sinabi na they took inspiration with Top Gun: Maverick but parang wala naman? Di IMAX worthy for me... Bradley Cooper & Milly Alcock looks great tho. The story was great. Hindi ganun kathick yung plot but maganda yung message: the choices you make defines you. Ok din yung political side of things. I remember this feeling. Ganito yung pakiramdam ko nung pagkalabas ng cinema after watching The Force Awakens. Di ko alam kung matutuwa ako or maiinis. Mixed feelings as of now. But who knows, it might grow on me. Henry Cavill pa rin for me. But yeah, past is past. 🤷‍♂️
