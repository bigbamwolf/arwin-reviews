Ne Zha 2 (2025) review

★★★★½
Rated 4.5/5
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2025-12-05
624 reviews · 1,550 films logged · 162,502 words
Ne Zha 2 carries the same vibes and aesthetic as the first film, but here the animation is much sharper and the details are more noticeable. You can definitely see the big change in terms of scale and the number of characters. The energy in this installment is much higher compared to the original. The camera movements are more fluid and the fur textures are surprisingly realistic. The film feels more polished and confident in its visual language. The guttural score in the second act really adds tension to the rising story. Ang lakas ng impact niya sa pacing at emotional weight. Maganda ang twist. There were signs pero hindi ko masyadong finocusan kasi mukha namang mabait at inosente talaga. Kaya pala napaka supportive niya kay Ne Zha at hindi niya napapansin na may demon spirit si Ne Zha, may hidden motives na pala! The transformation technique is a direct callback to the first movie which is a nice continuity and comedic touch. Ang ganda ng mga shots, parang painting. The artistry is very high and the compositions look intentional and cinematic. The emotional scene with his mother is sad and very heavy. Ramdam mo yung pain and love sabayan pa ng flashback scenes from the first movie. The crazy power up scene where he completes his body is a standout moment. Very hype and visually explosive. Yung part na enemies come together when faced with imminent death is a good moment. Sometimes danger forces unity and the movie showed that well. The tug of war escape to the cauldron is pure visual popcorn. Ang sarap panoorin. The stakes here are genuinely high. I forgot when was the last time I felt that level of hopelessness for the protagonists and not just fake danger. Dito ramdam mo talaga na pwedeng hindi sila magtagumpay. This sequel is darker than the first one and expands the lore in a way that feels natural. I can compare it to Empire Strikes Back and Dune Part Two in the sense that it deepens the world, raises the emotional stakes, and pushes the story into more mature territory.
