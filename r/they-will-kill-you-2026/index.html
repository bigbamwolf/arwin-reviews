They Will Kill You (2026) review

★★★★½
Rated 4.5/5
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-03-26
618 reviews · 1,544 films logged · 159,192 words
There are strong Kill Bill influences all over. The action zooms paired with those sharp snap sound effects instantly reminded me of Tarantino’s style. Even the theatrical gore feels very Kill Bill, exaggerated but satisfying. The story structure also follows that same approach, giving small callbacks and background moments for each character so they feel more fleshed out. Kinetic camera movements carry the whole film. It keeps everything energetic and chaotic, matching the nonstop violence and humor. The score is also consistently good, supporting the pace. The ballroom scene is easily one of the highlights. It feels like House of the Blue Leaves sequence in Kill Bill, but instead of samurai swords it is all about a burning axe. Ang ganda ng sequence na yun. The choreography, the chaos, and the visual style all come together perfectly. Lighting is also great throughout. It helps highlight the chaos while still making everything visually clear and stylish. There are also moments that feel like Wes Anderson style comedy. Especially in tight spaces where characters look like lab rats trapped in a maze. The framing and staging of those shots add a different kind of humor. I also like how the sisters reconnect within all the madness. That emotional thread works better here. Even the satanist angle feels more refined compared to Ready or Not 2. It still has that absurdity, but it is handled in a more engaging way.
