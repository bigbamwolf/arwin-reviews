Reminders of Him (2026) review

★★★★
Rated 4/5
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-03-12
602 reviews · 1,528 films logged · 156,200 words
Non spoiler thoughts (summary): A quiet story centered on forgiveness and the hope for redemption. The direction keeps things simple and controlled. The clean visual style allows the emotions and performances to stay at the forefront. What stays with me most is the emotional core of longing and healing. Even though there is a romantic thread, I feel the film is more focused on rebuilding broken connections and finding the courage to move forward after pain. ⚠️ Spoilers ahead. From here on, plot details are discussed. Read on if you’ve seen the film. If not, save this post and come back after. ⚠️ Forgiveness and the idea of redemption are at the center of the story. I would not say the film is beautifully lit in the traditional sense, but it feels intentionally lit. The lighting is simple and controlled, very clean in execution. It does not try too hard to be cinematic. I especially like the moments when the neon lights appear because they add a little personality to the film. But more than a romance, this story is about a mother longing for her daughter. That emotional core drives everything. The romance is there, but it is secondary to that deeper desire to reconnect and rebuild what was lost. When they finally meet, the moment feels like pure joy. After everything that happened and all the barriers in between, that reunion carries the emotional payoff the whole film has been building toward.
