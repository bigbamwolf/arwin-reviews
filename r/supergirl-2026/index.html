Supergirl 2026

½
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-06-25
I'd like to diss this movie. Okay, I just watched this because of Milly. She's just playing a drunken master most of the film. The world and set aesthetics feel very GOTG. I can also feel the Star Wars influence in the creature work. But overall, avoid! Boring! I did not have anything to latch onto. I dozed off from the second act to the third act. The pacing is so slow. Then when there's action, the choreography and camera work are just so hard to follow. The emotional beats did not land too. The coming of age and healing type of emotional beats did not appeal to me. There is no chemistry within the cast. It feels too forced.
