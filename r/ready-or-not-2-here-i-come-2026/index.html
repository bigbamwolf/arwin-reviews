Ready or Not 2: Here I Come (2026) review

★★
Rated 2/5
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-03-26
616 reviews · 1,542 films logged · 158,812 words
Funny and nonstop violent slasher. It just keeps throwing chaos, blood, and absurd situations one after another. Underneath all the violence, there is still that reconnecting with sister subplot. The bullshit bylaws of the family are actually one of the funniest parts for me. And that “this is Sparta” ending is just satisfying.
