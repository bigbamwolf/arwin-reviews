Retirement Plan 2024

★★★★½
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-03-14
It starts out fun and very relatable. The whole idea of a retirement bucket list immediately made me think about the things I also want to do someday, especially finally reading all the books I have been postponing for years. Then the tone slowly shifts into something more reflective. The film starts playful but gradually becomes more introspective about time, purpose, and the things we keep delaying. For such a short film it really packs a punch in the gut. It suddenly reminds me how many things there are to do in this beautiful life.
