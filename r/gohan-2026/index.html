Gohan 2026

★★★★
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-04-15
This film feels like a journey through different phases of Gohan’s life, where he experiences both kindness and hardship from the people around him. I liked how it shows the contrast between those who take advantage and those who genuinely care, and how those encounters slowly shape who he becomes. There’s also a quiet sense of growth running underneath, not just for him, but for the people he connects with along the way. I was smiling the whole time.It just has that feel good energy throughout. Cinematography is nice and shines especially in the circus scenes where the playfulness really shines. At times, the grading feels like a Nescafé commercial, warm, inviting, and a bit dreamy, especially in the moments with Aye Shin.
