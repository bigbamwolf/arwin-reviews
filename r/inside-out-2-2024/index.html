Inside Out 2 2024

★★★★
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2025-06-12
Inside Out 2 hits hardest in the moments where Riley can’t understand herself anymore. Ang ganda ng idea na one small confusion, one small impulse, can shake her whole identity. You can actually see the moment na nagcrumble yung “good person” identity niya, and that’s the part that stuck with me the most. Yung scene na she suddenly questions why she’s acting that way, bakit siya nagagalit, bakit siya may ginagawa na hindi niya maintindihan. That’s where the film is strongest. Ang relatable ng way na pinakita nila yung confusion ni Riley. Yung tipong may mga moments ka rin sa buhay na bigla mo naiisip: “Bakit ko nagawa yun?” “Ako ba talaga yun?” “Masama ba akong tao?” Ganun yung spiral na pinapakita ng movie, yung internal loop na bigla kang nagda-doubt sa sarili mo kahit hindi mo naman intensyon maging masama. Yung panic na baka ikaw yung pinakamalala even if you’re really just confused or overwhelmed. Ang galing ng film kasi navisualize niya yung ganung pakiramdam nang hindi OA, just honest and real. The collapsing identity tree is simple but effective. It is a clear symbol na she’s breaking down inside. And the way the new emotions argue with the old ones makes sense, hindi nila alam paano ihandle yung changes, so lalo lang gumugulo. The movie also captures that feeling of trying so hard to be good, pero hindi mo alam bakit nagfafail ka. When Riley starts doubting everything, even the memories na proof na mabuti siyang tao, sobrang totoo nung portrayal. Alam mo yung moment na kailangan mo maghold onto something someone once said about you para hindi ka malunod? That’s exactly what Riley does. Voice acting is great. Comedy is light lang. But the emotional beats are the real highlight. It’s not as heavy as the first film, but it’s very real when it comes to identity confusion and growing up. It’s a good continuation of Riley’s inner world and a nice look at how messy it feels when you’re trying to figure out who you are. Not perfect, not super deep, but the “good person identity crumble” scene makes the whole film worth it.
