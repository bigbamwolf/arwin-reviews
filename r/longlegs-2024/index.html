Longlegs (2024) review

★
Rated 1/5
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-10-26
Crime, Horror, Mystery · Directed by Osgood Perkins · 101 min
620 reviews · 1,546 films logged · 160,269 words
All that dread, but my heartbeat stayed completely normal. The investigation has that Fincher style approach. It reminded me of Se7en (1995), especially with how methodical everything feels as clues slowly connect and the investigators try to understand someone deeply disturbing. But Longlegs uses that procedural style more effectively for atmosphere than actual tension for me. The cinematography and sound design are actually good. They create an eerie vibe. I love how the framing sometimes leaves so much empty space around the characters. The sound design also keeps this constant uneasiness underneath even when nothing is happening. But then the story itself is not that creepy nor scary. Didn’t have a lasting effect. Did not even have my heart pounding. That is probably my biggest problem. The filmmaking keeps telling my senses that I should feel disturbed, but the actual story never scared me enough to match how creepy the movie looks and sounds. Verdict: Failed Watched on Netflix.
