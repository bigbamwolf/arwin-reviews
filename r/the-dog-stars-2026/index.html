The Dog Stars (2026) review

★★½
Rated 2.5/5
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-08-27
610 reviews · 1,536 films logged · 157,710 words
This is just another Ridley Scott's side mission. Nothing new. Lesson learned: The grass isn’t always greener on the other side. I have to praise the cinematography because of its serene aerial sceneries. The landscapes are beautiful and peaceful. It feels like the calm before the storm. The day-for-night cinematography is also noticeable. It kind of looks like Nope (2022). There are so many ad placements, especially Carhartt. I couldn’t stop seeing it. The movie is really slow and boring, and it only ramps up after 1 hour and 12 minutes, when Josh Brolin suddenly switches into full-blown Sicario mode.
