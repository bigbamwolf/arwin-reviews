Enter the Dragon (1973) review

★
Rated 1/5
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-02-19
Action, Crime, Thriller · Directed by Robert Clouse · 99 min
624 reviews · 1,550 films logged · 162,502 words
Non spoiler thoughts (summary): A more polished and cinematic evolution of Bruce Lee’s formula, even if the emotional hook feels lighter. The film refines what came before by using real locations, clearer fight staging, and more deliberate cinematography that gives it a bigger screen presence. Bruce Lee feels sharper and more athletic, and the story shifts toward a spy infiltration mixed with tournament structure rather than pure revenge drama. The action is cleaner and more readable, though I connect less emotionally compared to Fist of Fury since it plays more like a mission than a personal story. ⚠️ Spoilers ahead. From here on, plot details are discussed. Read on if you’ve seen the film. If not, save this post and come back after. ⚠️ This still carries the same DNA as Fist of Fury but now it feels more refined and cinematic. The film finally becomes alive because many scenes are shot on real locations instead of obvious studio interiors. The lenses also feel more deliberate, producing soft circular bokeh instead of that flatter look from earlier films. Bruce Lee also looks different here. He is still ripped but leaner, less bulky, almost more athletic than muscular, and his face looks slimmer which adds to the sharper screen presence. The movie plays like a mix of spy film and Mortal Kombat style tournament structure. There is investigation, infiltration, secret island villain base, then rounds of martial arts fights layered into it. I honestly could not care much about most characters here. I sympathized more with Bruce Lee’s motivations in Fist of Fury, while this one feels more mission driven than emotionally driven. The fights themselves are clearly improved. They are better lit, cleaner, and no longer rely on sped up motion that accidentally turns serious moments into comedy. The final fight: Bruce Lee vs. Chinese Wolverine wanabe
