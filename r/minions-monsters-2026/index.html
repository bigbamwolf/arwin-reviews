Minions & Monsters 2026

★★★
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-07-09
Chaotically fun. It is super easy to get swept up by the happy vibes because the Minions are just so funny and adorable. Their chaotic energy carries most of the movie, and it is hard not to laugh whenever they are rowdy on screen. I also like the black and white sequences. They add a nice visual touch and stand out from the rest of the film. The story is so so, but forgivable since it's a children's movie. It knows its audience, and its biggest strength is simply being fun, lighthearted, and entertaining.
