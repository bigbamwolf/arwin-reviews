Killers of the Flower Moon (2023) review

★★★★
Rated 4/5
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2025-10-21
610 reviews · 1,536 films logged · 157,710 words
Detective style mystery, may pagka Knives Out with the world vibe of Sherlock Holmes and the grit of Peaky Blinders. The use of anamorphic lenses gives it that cinematic stretch and emotional depth, perfect for a story that unfolds slowly and deliberately. The cinematography and color grading are stunning, napakaganda lalo na sa mga sunrise at sunset scenes habang nagdadasal ang mga natives. There’s something poetic about those frames. Every silhouette feels spiritual, a visual contrast to the corruption and greed happening underneath. Super slow burn siya. Parang To Kill a Mockingbird with the litigation pacing, pero mas mabigat dahil sa historical context. The story takes its time, showing how deep the manipulation and systemic control run. The musical score stands out, particularly the low bass tones that hum beneath key moments. Parang heartbeat ng tension. Every note adds mystery and quiet dread. Leonardo dissolved into Ernest’s character. Nakakainis si Ernest dito. Frustrating to watch. Uto uto, walang sariling desisyon, sunud sunuran sa uncle niya. Pero deep down, alam mong mahal niya si Mollie. Weak minded lang talaga siya, torn between conscience and loyalty. I could really feel Ernest’s pain when his daughter died. No need for dramatic monologues, the silence and his face already said everything. I honestly believe Ernest loved Mollie. He just didn’t have the strength to fight for her. The writing does a great job showing how the white settlers slowly built their wealth through manipulation, marriage, and exploitation of the natives’ estate. Smart storytelling that captures how greed hides behind generosity. It’s also a strong reflection on how outsiders took power and flipped the story to make themselves look like the heroes. Production design and set authenticity are believable. You can tell the budget is massive because of the scale and the accuracy of every scene, from costumes to landscapes to the small town structures Available on Apple TV+
