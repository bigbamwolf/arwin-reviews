The Singers 2025

★★½
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-03-15
The lens choices, lighting, and color grading are really nicely done here. I especially like the low key style of lighting. The shadows give the scenes a nice depth and mood without making everything look too dark. The visuals feel carefully controlled, letting the faces and emotions stand out more than the environment. It gives the film a very intimate atmosphere. And that use of Unchained Melody at the end was chef’s kiss. It ties the emotional tone together beautifully.
