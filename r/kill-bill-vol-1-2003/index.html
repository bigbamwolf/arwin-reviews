Kill Bill: Vol. 1 2003

★★★★★
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-05-05
In my memory, this felt like a fast paced action film, but on rewatch it plays more like a slow burn. But I still love it! The revenge story is strong, and the filmmaking is on another level. The visual style, the composition, everything feels artistic. Uma Thurman is also incredibly magnetic here. She carries the film with her presence. Definitely worth a rewatch!
