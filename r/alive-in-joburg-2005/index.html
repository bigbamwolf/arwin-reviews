Alive in Joburg 2005

★★★½
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-04-11
Good short film that really works as a pitch piece. I can clearly see how it laid the foundation for one of the most memorable sci-fi alien films to date. It already captures that raw, documentary style and social commentary, making the world feel believable even with aliens in it. Such a strong proof of concept!
