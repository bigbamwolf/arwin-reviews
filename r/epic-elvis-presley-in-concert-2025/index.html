EPiC: Elvis Presley in Concert 2025

★★★★
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-02-21
I can really feel his passion and pride in his craft. The director already did a great job in the movie version, but this one is on a completely on a different level! His showmanship is impeccable. Even if I do not know most of the songs, my feet and knees start moving while watching. He feels literally and figuratively bigger than life on the IMAX screen. I can see how physically and mentally present he is with his band, his back up singers, and the audience. He moves like a conductor leading an orchestra, controlling energy and emotion in real time. My last concert docufilm before was One Direction, and the difference in stage command is massive. No offense to Austin Butler, but I realized nothing can match the real Elvis. The restoration of the footage is great, crystal clear audio and visuals. I ended the film with newfound respect and admiration for Elvis Presley.
