The Punisher: One Last Kill 2026

★★★★
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-05-28
A depressed, sad, tired, and lonely Frank Castle was perfectly portrayed by Jon Bernthal. Too real and too painful to watch. Love the unapologetic grittiness, and it reminded me why I loved his show from 2017. Wow, almost a decade already! Weird music choice using La Vie en Rose during a violent scene. Feels almost sarcastic with a man clearly not seeing life in pink. Satisfying violence though! Especially with the pen scene. Cannot go wrong with a pen in an action scene. Glad Disney did not hold back. It is really a bloody affair. It woke up my sleepy and bored ass.
