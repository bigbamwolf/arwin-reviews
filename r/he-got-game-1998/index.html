He Got Game (1998) review

★★★
Rated 3/5
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-11-07
648 reviews · 1,574 films logged · 172,352 words
The agent scene is basically the serpent tempting Jesus with a sneaker deal. That scene is my favorite. Top notch sales pitch. It feels like the serpent tempting Jesus. He offers him everything he wants, while the real cost is hidden underneath. This film shows the cost of stardom. The bloodsuckers. The leeches. Everyone wants a piece of him. His talent makes him valuable, but that same value attracts agents, schools, women, friends, and basically anyone who suddenly sees something they can get from him. While watching, I was really just waiting for that emotional payoff between father and son. But I guess this is how some father and son relationships are. The revelation of the purpose behind tough love is only realized later through life experience. Jesus starts understanding his father more once he experiences pressure, manipulation, responsibility, and adulthood for himself. I was expecting something more confrontational. A big speech. An apology. Some kind of emotional climax. But their reconciliation is quieter than that. Like some sort of whisper only a heart can hear. Not everything between a father and son needs to be spoken out loud for understanding to finally happen. Verdict: Watchable Watched on Disney+
