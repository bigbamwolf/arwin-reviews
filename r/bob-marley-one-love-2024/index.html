Bob Marley: One Love (2024) review

★★
Rated 2/5
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-10-29
Biography, Drama, Music · Directed by Reinaldo Marcus Green · 107 min
626 reviews · 1,552 films logged · 162,974 words
Bob Marley made peace sound revolutionary. The music and their movement about unity, love, and peace hit so hard. I did not recognize Kingsley Ben-Adir. He completely disappeared into Bob Marley, from the accent to the mannerisms and the way he carries himself while performing. Lashana Lynch disappeared into her role too, especially with her accent. This is completely different from how I saw her in No Time to Die (2021), where I did not really connect with her presence. Here, I barely even thought about Lashana Lynch while watching her. I also like how the performances make the music feel communal rather than just a concert. Whenever everyone starts moving and singing together, I can really feel why messages about unity, love, and peace could become something much bigger than just lyrics. The downside is that the storytelling feels too conventional, and some of the dramatic moments aren't engaging enough. I appreciate the music and performances, but I expected to feel more emotionally connected to Bob Marley's journey. I think the acting is much better than the film itself. Verdict: Weak Watched on Prime
