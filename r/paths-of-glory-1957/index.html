Paths of Glory 1957

★★★½
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-04-02
The scale of no man’s land is great, but the sound feels blown out and harsh at times. This is more of a courtroom drama than a full blown war film, and I actually find that interesting. Instead of just focusing on battle, it exposes the ugly ego and mindset inside the military. Power, pride, and self preservation take over so much that they forget how to be human and compassionate.
