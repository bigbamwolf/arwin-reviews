Avengers: Endgame (2019) review

★★★★★
Rated 5/5
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2019-04-24
624 reviews · 1,550 films logged · 162,502 words
This film hits on a level that is hard to replicate. The moment that really got me is how perfectly timed the score is. That “Portals” scene alone feels like pure emotion. I wish I could wipe my memory so I can experience that feeling again. The score made me feel satisfied for the heroic act, but at the same time left me feeling empty for the loss. Every day I wake up realizing that there would be no more appearance of him in any Marvel film in the future. Thank you for the last sacrifice. You made the world a whole lot safer. I love you 3000! What a way to end the Infinity Saga with this. This made me root for Earth’s mightiest heroes more. They have gone separate ways, fought divided, then won together. Finally a perfect closure to the characters I loved for eleven years. Thank you Russo Brothers for a proper send off to our beloved characters. Team Cap and Team Iron Man no more. Only Team Avengers.
