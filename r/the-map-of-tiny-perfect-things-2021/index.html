The Map of Tiny Perfect Things 2021

★★
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-08-29
A light and sincere story that feels like a young adult novel infused with time loop DNA. It has the warmth, awkwardness, and bittersweet emotional honesty of a coming of age romance, while using the repeating day concept to explore connection, regret, and appreciating the small moments in life. They even name drop Groundhog Day and Edge of Tomorrow. Wink wink!
