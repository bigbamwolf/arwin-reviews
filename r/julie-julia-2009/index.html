Julie & Julia (2009) review

★★
Rated 2/5
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-02-11
Biography, Drama, Romance · Directed by Nora Ephron · 123 min
600 reviews · 1,526 films logged · 155,998 words
Non spoiler thoughts (summary): I experience this as a relatable story about creative persistence and passion, where the emotional core connects with me more than the performances. Even when certain character choices distract me, the message about commitment, growth, and loving the process still lands. Full review: I honestly get annoyed by Meryl Streep’s voice here. It sounds high pitched, almost Mickey Mouse like, and it feels nasal, like someone talking while congested nose. Everything seems to come from the throat instead of natural breathing. The hairstyle does not help either. It adds to the exaggerated presentation, which makes her performance feel distracting to me even though I understand it is a character choice. On the other side, I resonate strongly with Julie’s journey. Watching her write a blog and fall in love with the process hits close to home. It reminds me of how I feel when I watch films and post my reviews online. That sense of documenting a diary, sharing thoughts, and finding identity through creative expression feels very personal. The film speaks about persistence. It shows how messy progress can be and how plans rarely unfold perfectly. Still, the message is clear to me. Even when things feel frustrating or slow, the important part is to keep going. The story frames passion as something that grows through repetition, mistakes, and stubborn dedication. Overall, I experience Julie & Julia as a simple but relatable reminder about commitment to what I love. Despite my distraction with the vocal performance, the emotional thread about passion, discipline, and not quitting still lands.
