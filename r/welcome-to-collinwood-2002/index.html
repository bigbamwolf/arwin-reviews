Welcome to Collinwood (2002) review

★★
Rated 2/5
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-09-27
602 reviews · 1,528 films logged · 156,200 words
A light and funny heist movie where almost everything that can go wrong eventually does. The fun comes less from the heist itself and more from watching this group constantly deal with one problem after another. The incompetence and bad luck become part of the entertainment.
