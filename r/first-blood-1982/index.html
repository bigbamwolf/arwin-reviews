First Blood (1982) review

★★★★
Rated 4/5
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-01-18
624 reviews · 1,550 films logged · 162,502 words
Non spoiler thoughts (summary): First Blood is a grounded, physical action film that proves how effective practical filmmaking can be when paired with strong thematic weight. The tension is built through music, atmosphere, and escalating confrontations rather than spectacle for its own sake. Action sequences feel dangerous because they rely on real locations, practical effects, and physical exhaustion instead of artificial polish. Visually, the film makes strong use of lighting and lens choices to heighten realism and claustrophobia. Beneath the action, the story explores authority, ego, and the difficulty of reintegration after war, showing how small abuses of power can spiral into devastating consequences. It is less about heroics and more about what happens when a broken man is pushed past his limits. Spoilers ahead. Read on if you’ve seen the film. From here on, plot details are discussed. Ang angas ng jail break. Classic action movie trope done right. Talo niya lahat ng pulis kahit mag isa lang siya. Ano ba naman laban ng mga ego driven police laban sa isang Medal of Honor Vietnam veteran? Maganda rin ang musical score. It constantly builds tension for the scene. Ang galing nung hunting sequence with the helicopter. That level of practicality is what makes old action movies incomparable with today’s CGI heavy films. Ramdam mo yung realism ng chopper, yung danger ng terrain, at yung physical exhaustion ng characters. Walang artificial polish, everything feels realistic. The jungle cat and mouse aesthetic feels like Predator, but this time American vs American. Ang ganda ng cinematography sa mining cave sequence. Torch lighting lang ang source. And yung explosion sa gasoline station reminds me of Jarhead and There Will Be Blood. Galing ng production design, talagang sinisira ang set. Real explosions, real debris, real gunfire hits. Lens choices are also great. The anamorphic lenses work especially well in medium and close up shots. All these troubles could have been avoided kung pinakawalan na lang siya ng sheriff, exactly as Rambo suggested. It was just escalated by ego and abuse of authority. At its core, this film is about how hard it is for soldiers to find their footing as civilians. The war has ended, but their war is still ongoing in their heads. First Blood is about what happens when a broken man with PTSD is pushed too far by people who refuse to listen. Making small power trips turning into full scale violence.
