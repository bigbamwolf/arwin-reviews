Whiplash 2013

★★★★★
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-03-28
Still carries the same fear and tension even in this shorter version. Even in this early form, it is really, really great. The lighting and camera work are still great. It may not have Linus Sandgren’s signature color intensity yet, but the visual style is already strong. The shadows, the focus on faces, and the tight framing already create that suffocating pressure.
