Curve 2016

★★★★½
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-04-11
Sound effects and acting are both great. The situation itself makes me nervous, like I am about to fall with her. The fear is breath taking. It reminds me of Fall 2022 in terms of how it builds fear from height and helplessness. Super tense!!
