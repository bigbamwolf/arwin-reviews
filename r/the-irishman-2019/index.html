The Irishman (2019) review

★½
Rated 1.5/5
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-05-15
Biography, Crime, Drama · Directed by Martin Scorsese · 209 min
602 reviews · 1,528 films logged · 156,200 words
It has the same vibes as Goodfellas, The Godfather, and The Departed with its mafia centered story and criminal world. The de-aging tech is insane! Scorsese’s directing and the cinematography are still great as expected, but I just could not fully attach myself emotionally to the story. Maybe I am starting to experience gangster fatigue after watching so many films in this genre. For me, the film feels less about crime itself and more about the life cycle of being a gangster. From the rise and formation of that lifestyle, to its slow degradation and emptiness as time passes.
