Ivan's Childhood 1962

★★
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-04-09
Ivan’s childhood is beautifully shot. The cinematography really stands out. The contrast between the dreamlike imagery and the harsh reality of war is visually striking. But despite how good it looks, I just could not connect with the story and the characters. I appreciate the craft, but I stayed emotionally distant the whole time.
