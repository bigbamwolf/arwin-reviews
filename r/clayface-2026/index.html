Clayface (2026) review

★★★★
Rated 4/5
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-10-30
629 reviews · 1,555 films logged · 164,190 words
Went in just to kill time. Came out impressed by the kill streak. Not really on my watchlist. The trailer didn’t pull me in either. I just watched this while waiting for my car’s PMS to be completed to pass the time. So I basically walked in with very little expectation, which probably made the surprise even better. What stood out to me first was the red color motif that kept showing up, I love how the red keeps finding its way into the imagery, giving the film an instantly recognizable visual identity. The lighting is good. Overall, the cinematography is great. I didn’t expect this to be this cinematic. I’m not familiar with the character, so naturally, it became intriguing once his abilities were revealed. Basically, just like Mystique from X-Men, except the transformations here become much more disturbing because of the body horror. The musical score is also good at times. There are moments where it really helps make the transformation and revenge sequences feel grander. It was so uncomfortable to watch during the first half, but I got used to the body horror. The CGI and prosthetics look great when working together. They just blend smoothly. That combination really helps because the transformations still have texture instead of looking completely CG. The production design is also good, gritty and cohesive with Batman’s world. The dirty streets, smoke, shadows, and overall atmosphere feel like they belong in a city where someone like Batman could be operating somewhere nearby. Great origin story. Insane kill streak. Fantastic revenge movie. Glad I watched it. Verdict: Well Made Watched at Glorietta Cinema 3 in Dolby Atmos.
