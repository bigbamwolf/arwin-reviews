The Island (2005) review

★★★★
Rated 4/5
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-04-21
Action, Sci-Fi, Thriller · Directed by Michael Bay · 136 min
616 reviews · 1,542 films logged · 158,812 words
Non spoiler thoughts (summary): The film carries Michael Bay’s signature DNA, that constant sense of imminent threat and high tension danger. There is a strong cat and mouse energy throughout. The car chases especially feel very Transformers like, chaotic, destructive, and filled with crashing vehicles everywhere. What I like is that the CGI blends well with practical effects, so the action still feels grounded despite the scale. ⚠️ Spoilers ahead. From here on, plot details are discussed. Read on if you’ve seen the film. If not, save this post and come back after. ⚠️ The film also raises an interesting question, do clone lives matter? That idea gives a deeper layer beneath all the action. The final liberation shot is genuinely triumphant. After all the chaos, it gives that satisfying sense of freedom and release.
