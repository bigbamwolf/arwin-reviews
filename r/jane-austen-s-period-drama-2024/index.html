Jane Austen's Period Drama 2024

★★★★
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-03-29
Well it is actually funny when it finally clicked why that is the title. I think the film works as a satire on how men can be so dumb to marry a woman without really knowing who she is or what she is capable of. I would honestly love to see this expanded into a feature length film. It feels like there is still so much more that could be explored with that concept and humor.
