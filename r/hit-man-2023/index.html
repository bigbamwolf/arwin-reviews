Hit Man 2023

★★
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-04-28
Richard Linklater is really great at creating a complicated yet very attractive woman. The kind of character who feels charming, unpredictable, and morally messy at the same time. She is not just written to be beautiful, but someone with danger and personality, which makes her more interesting to watch.
