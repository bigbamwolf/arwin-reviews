Jennifer's Body (2009) review

★★★½
Rated 3.5/5
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-11-01
640 reviews · 1,566 films logged · 168,702 words
Hot take: Amanda is hotter than Megan. ⚠️ Spoilers ahead. From here on, plot details are discussed. Read on if you've seen the film. If not, save this post and come back after. ⚠️ But their kissing scene, oh my! Didn’t see that coming!!! The lighting is what makes the atmosphere strong. There is something about the way darkness, color, and practical locations are used that gives the movie this seductive but dangerous atmosphere. The ritual at the falls is the highlight for me. The whole sequence works. The location, lighting, staging, performances, and atmosphere make it feel like the moment when the movie fully embraced supernatural horror. Ahmet and Jennifer’s encounter is another highlight. I love the lighting, staging, and tension there. I also love the wide shot of the townhouse when the Colin goes to the address Jennifer gave him. The composition and that eerie, empty suburban image are so good. It is such a simple shot, but the emptiness immediately makes the place feel wrong. The third act feels like a young adult book coming to life. It has almost everything I associate with that kind of supernatural YA story: high school drama, friendship problems, romance, supernatural powers, a teenage heroine finally standing up for herself, and a final confrontation where all those elements collide. It feels heightened in a way that actually fits the movie. Actually a good and satisfying ending. I love Needy finally fighting back. And once she gains powers, the ending becomes even more satisfying because Jennifer’s influence does not simply disappear. Needy takes what happened to her and turns it against the people responsible. Verdict: Interesting Watched on Disney+
