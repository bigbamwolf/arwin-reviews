Good Luck, Have Fun, Don't Die (2025) review

★★★★
Rated 4/5
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-09-02
624 reviews · 1,550 films logged · 162,502 words
Feels like an anthology episode of Black Mirror. Different characters, strange technology, absurd situations, and a story that slowly reveals how fucked up this world actually is. It has that same feeling of using sci-fi and dark comedy to exaggerate something uncomfortable about real life. This might be one of the darkest comedies I’ve felt, especially with the school shooting and the parents talking about their kid dying four times. The characters are so detached and out of touch with their own feelings that the tragedy somehow becomes funny and disturbing at the same time. The ending feels like Avengers: Infinity War. It has that same sense of watching everything reach a massive breaking point, only for the movie to leave you sitting there processing what the hell just happened. Makes me want to do a rewatch.
