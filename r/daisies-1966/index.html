Daisies 1966

★★
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-03-28
Too creative and artistic for a 60s film. It feels way ahead of its time. The editing, the visuals, and the overall style feel bold and experimental, like it is breaking rules on purpose. I can imagine how provocative this must have been back then. The way it plays with structure and behavior feels rebellious, almost like it is challenging what is considered acceptable.
