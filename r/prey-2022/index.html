Prey 2022

★★★
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-08-26
There are some cool scenic, cinematic wide shots that I think were shot in real locations. If yes, then I really appreciate that. It is kind of a slow burn at the start, which made me quite sleepy because of the dim lighting, but yeah, it served its purpose in establishing Naru’s hunting instincts, observation skills, and ability to adapt before throwing her against something far beyond anything she has hunted before. And I’m glad that the first half and even much of the second half barely showed the Predator. So when it was finally revealed properly, it felt terrifying.
