The Hunt 2020

★★★½
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-09-05
I like it. Decent action with a satirical flavor. It feels like it is playing with the same tropes as The Purge, Ready or Not, where wealthy or powerful people turn human suffering into some kind of game or entertainment. It also feels like Saw, but loaded with almost every social and political commentary from the last decade: race, veganism, the Second Amendment, immigration, feminism, climate change, and even elites using their country’s political power for personal business interests.
