Kita Kita (2017) review

★★★★
Rated 4/5
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-09-26
624 reviews · 1,550 films logged · 162,502 words
Ikaw ang nagpabago ng pagtingin ko sa buhay ko. Alam mo ang labo mo. Nung nakakakita ka, di mo ako nakita. Nung nabulag ka saka mo lang ako nakita. Poetic, funny, and full of heart. Kita Kita has this simple charm that makes it easy to settle into, but underneath the humor is something genuinely warm and emotional. The sincerity of the characters and the way the story balances comedy with tenderness are what make it work for me. I miss Japan!!!
