Fall 2: Deadpoint 2026

★★★
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-09-10
There are people who just don’t know how to quit, right? I found myself holding my breath during certain scenes, just like in the first one. I like the POV shots because they really trigger my fear of heights. There are also some Final Destination-esque moments where the camera puts so much emphasis on certain objects that may or may not cause an accident. The feeling is almost like watching someone do Free Solo, then finally letting out a sigh of relief once they are back somewhere safe. It was actually a suspenseful ride. I also appreciate the main character’s character development. From being a pussy to becoming a strong, brave woman. From broken to healed. It is basically a journey from fear to courage.
