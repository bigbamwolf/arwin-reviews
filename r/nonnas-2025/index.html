Nonnas (2025) review

★★★½
Rated 3.5/5
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-10-10
Comedy · Directed by Stephen Chbosky · 111 min
626 reviews · 1,552 films logged · 162,974 words
Light and full of heart. This really fits my positive outlook on life, especially my lane of family, legacy, and business ambition. It kind of reminded me of Mamma Mia! (2008) because of the warmth, the older generation carrying so much history, memories attached to food and family, and this sense that life does not simply end when you get older. There is still joy, purpose, friendship, and something new to look forward to. Then there is the Chef (2014) side of it because of the business side, where food becomes more than just food. It becomes a way to rebuild purpose, connect with people, and create something meaningful that can eventually become part of your legacy. Watched on Netflix.
