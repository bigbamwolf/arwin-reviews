Terminator 2: Judgment Day (1991) review

★
Rated 1/5
Reviewed by Arwin Edward Bagaslao · 2026-03-21
Action, Adventure, Sci-Fi · Directed by James Cameron · 137 min
610 reviews · 1,536 films logged · 157,710 words
The opening sequence is nice. It gives me that old school large scale war vibe, parang Rogue One climax type faceoff but with that classic apocalyptic feel. The action and set pieces are definitely bigger here. Everything feels more ambitious, mas malaki ang scale compared to the first film. But strangely, I do not really feel the danger as much even if John is supposed to be at stake. I was not as engaged. The film leans heavily into spectacle and action, but it loses some of the tension that made the first one more gripping for me. I honestly could not stop comparing this to James Cameron’s Aliens, especially with the mother and child dynamic. That emotional core was so strong in Aliens with Ripley and Newt. I genuinely cared about them. Here with Sarah and John, I did not feel that same connection. The relationship is there, but it did not hit me emotionally the same way. In the end, I see this as a bigger and more action driven sequel, but for me it sacrifices some of the heart and tension that made the first film and even Aliens more effective.
